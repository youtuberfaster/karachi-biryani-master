🍖 Nehari Masala Banane Ka Tarika

Asslam Alikum

Aj hum ap ko Nehari Masala banana seekha rahe hain — ye wo masala hai jo bary level par nehari banane wale use karte hain! ✨

🔸 Ingredients (اجزاء):

  • 500 gram pisi lal mirch powder
  • 500 gram dhaniya (sabit)
  • 125 gram haldi powder
  • 250 gram sonf
  • 150 gram sonth powder
  • 60 gram sabit zeera
  • 25 gram teez patta
  • 25 gram kasoori methi
  • 60 gram kala zeera
  • 250 gram chicken powder
  • 10 gram jaifal
  • 10 gram javetri
  • 10 gram dar cheeni
  • 10 gram long
  • 10 gram kali mirch
  • 10 gram badi elaichi
  • 50 gram pepli
  • 25 gram zardy ka colour

🔸 Nehari Masala banane ka tareeqa:

Step 1: Tamam masalay mix karein

  • In tamam masalon ko achi tarah mix karein

Step 2: Grand karein

  • Achi tarah grind karein aur powder bana lein

Step 3: Sabit masalay shamil karein

  • Masala grind karne ke baad is mein 30 gram sabit zeera aur 30 gram kali mirch (sabit) shamil kar dein
💡 Ap ka behtreen Nehari Masala ready hai!

🔸 استعمال کرنے کا طریقہ:

  • Jab bhi nehari banayein — sirf namak shamil karein aur ye wala masala shamil karein

✅ Ap ka behtreen Nehari Masala ready hai!
