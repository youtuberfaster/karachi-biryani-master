🥩 Karachi Beef Pulao

Asslam Alikum

Aj hum ap ko Karachi ke bohat he mashoor gosht dar yakhni Beef Pulao ki recipe seekha rahe hain!

🔸 Ingredients (اجزاء):

  • 1kg sela rice Kainat 1121 (1 ghanty ke liye pani mein bhego dein)
  • 1kg beef choti boti (hadi wala beef bhi use kar sakte hain)

🔸 Step 1: Beef ko tayyar karein (Yakhni banayein)

Pateli mein 1kg beef daalein aur ye masalajat shamil karein:

  • 2 liter pani
  • 3 adad badiyan ke phool
  • 2 tukray dar cheeni
  • 1 tea spoon kali mirch
  • 1 tea spoon long
  • 1 tea spoon zeera
  • 1 table spoon adrak lehsan ka paste

✅ Normal pateli mein:

  • Tamam masalajat shamil kar ke neeche chulha jala lein
  • Tez aanch par ubal ane tak pakayein
  • Jab ubal aajaye to chulha low kar dein
  • Dhakkan laga kar 40 minute tak dum dein

✅ Pressure cooker mein (optional):

  • 1 liter pani shamil karein
  • Tez aanch par pakayein
  • Seeti ane ke baad chulha low kar dein
  • 20 minute ka dum laga dein

🔸 Step 2: Pulao ki gravy banayein

Pateli mein 100 gram oil daalein aur chulha jala lein. Is mein ye sab masalayat shamil karein:

  • 8 adad imli ke dany shamil karein
  • 1 lemon (gol bareek cut kiya hua) shamil karein
  • 2 table spoon adrak lehsan shamil karein

Inhein 1 minute tak bhoon lein

  • 300 gram tamatar shamil karein aur mix karein
  • 1.5 table spoon namak shamil karein
  • 5 table spoon hari mirch ka paste shamil karein
  • 1 cup pani shamil karein

Achi tarah bhoonai karein:

  • Tamatar mash ho jayein
  • Dany daar gravy ban jaye
  • Oil upar aajaye
💡 Secret Pulao Masala: Ab is mein 40 gram pulao masala shamil karein ge.
Pulao masala bohat he secret hota hai — Karachi ke sab se bary mashoor pakwan centre ye masla aur miqdar kisi ko nahi batate.
Aap ki saholat ke liye pulao masala banane ki recipe website mein shamil ki gayi hai — ap dekh kar ghar mein pulao masala bana sakte hain.
  • Halki aanch par pulao masala daal kar bhoonein — masala jalna nahi chahiye (iska khas khyal rakhein)
  • Ab is mein 1/2 gathi dhaniya, 1/2 gathi podina shamil karein aur mix karein

🔸 Step 3: Beef shamil karein

  • Jo beef hum ne 40 minute tak boil kiya hai — sirf beef shamil karein ge (yakhni alag rakhein)
  • 5 minute tak achi tarah bhoonein
  • 💡 Agar pani ki zaroorat paray to thori si yakhni daalein — plain pani nahi use karna
  • Jab boti achi tarah masala pakar le — raseli aur juicy ho jaye

🔸 Step 4: Yakhni shamil karein

  • Ab is mein 1 liter se thora zyada yakhni daalein (jis mein beef boil kiya tha)
  • 💡 Beef ko boil karte waqt hum ne 2 liter pani is liye shamil kiya tha — jab masala bana rahe hain to thore se pani ki zaroorat parti hai, is liye sirf yakhni wala pani istemal karte hain
  • Yakhni shamil karne ke baad chulha high kar dein — taake yakhni mein ubal aajaye

🔸 Step 5: Namak check karein

  • Jab yakhni mein ubal aajaye to namak chak kar lein
  • Agar namak kam hai to aur shamil karein
  • ⚠️ Is muqam par namak thora sa teez hona zaroori hai

🔸 Step 6: Rice shamil karein aur dum dein

  • Ab is mein rice shamil karein
  • Jab rice shamil ho jaye to chak karein — rice yakhni mein achi tarah doobna zaroori hai
  • Chulha medium rakhein
  • Khara chamch chalate rahein — chamch ko gol gol nahi ghumana
  • Jab rice yakhni pee le aur 1 cup yakhni bache to chulha bilkul low kar dein
  • Dhakkan laga kar koi wazan upar rakh dein
  • 15 minute ka dum laga dein

✅ Ap ke 1kg Beef Pulao ready hai!
