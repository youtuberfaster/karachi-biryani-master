🐔 چکن چنے کا پلاؤ

Asslam Alikum

Aj hum ap ko Karachi ke mashoor Chicken Chana Pulao ki recipe seekha rahe hain

🔸 Ingredients (اجزاء):

  • 1kg chicken
  • 1kg sela rice Kainat 1121 (1 ghanta bhego kar rakhein)
  • 300 gram Lahori chana (80% boil hona chahiye)

🔸 Masalajat (ایک پلیٹ میں بنا لیں):

  • 200 gram brown piyaz (ya 200 gram kachi piyaz bareek cut kar ke pateli mein brown karein)
  • 50 gram hari mirch ka paste
  • 8 adad badiyan ke phool
  • 2 table spoon zeera
  • 1 table spoon kali mirch
  • 1 table spoon long
  • 1 table spoon kuti mirch
  • 1.5 table spoon namak
  • 8 adad aloo bukhara
  • 3 tukray dar cheeni
  • 2 pattay teez pat
  • 2 table spoon adrak lehsan ka paste
  • 200 gram tamatar (bareek cut)
  • 200 gram dahi
  • 4 adad badi elaichi
  • 4 adad choti elaichi
  • 1kg chicken

🔸 Pulao banane ka tareeqa:

Step 1: Masala bhonain

  • Pateli mein 200 gram oil daalein
  • Tamam masalajat (jo hum ne pehle bana liye hain) sab ek saath daal kar bhonain
  • Tamatar aur dahi apna pani chor dein ge — dany daar gravy banayein
  • Jab oil nikal aaye to us mein 1kg chicken shamil karein aur mix karein

Step 2: Chicken pakayein

  • 1 glass pani daal kar achi tarah bhonain
  • Jab chicken 50% gal jaye to boil chana shamil karein aur mix karein

Step 3: Pani aur masala daalein

  • Pateli mein 1/2 liter pani shamil karein
  • Jab pani josh mein aajaye to:
  • 1 table spoon sabit dhaniya shamil karein
  • 1 table spoon sonf shamil karein
  • 1 tea spoon garam masala shamil karein
  • 10 adad sabit hari mirch shamil karein

Step 4: Rice shamil karein

  • Ab rice shamil karein
  • Rice mukammal doobna chahiye achi tarah
  • Is waqt namak chak kar lein — agar kam ho to aur shamil karein
  • Yaad rakhein: Namak halka tez hona chahiye

Step 5: Kishmish aur dum

  • Jab rice pani pee lein aur 1 cup ke barabar pani bache to:
  • 100 gram kishmish shamil karein aur mix karein
  • Dhakkan laga dein
  • Chulha bilkul low kar dein
  • 10 minute ka dum laga dein

✅ Ap ke paas Karachi ka mashoor aur mazaydar Chicken Chana Pulao ready hai!
