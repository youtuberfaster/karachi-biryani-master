🥘 Karachi Chicken Biryani Recipe

Asslam Alikum

Aj hum ap ko Karachi ke sab se mashoor lazeez tasty biryani ke resy seekhain gy

🔸 1kg Chicken Biryani

Sab se pehly 1kg rice Kainat 11/21 sela 2 ghanty ke liye pani mein bhego dein.

Biryani mein shamil honay wala ejza:

  • 1kg rice
  • 1kg chicken
  • 1kg aloo

Masalajat:

Ek plate mein masala bana lein:

  • 1/2 spoon kali mirch (aadha spoon)
  • 1/2 spoon long (aadha spoon)
  • 1 spoon zeera
  • 6 adad badiyan ke phool
  • 3 tukray dar cheeni (1 inch ka 1 tukra ho)
  • 1 spoon lal mirch powder
  • 1/2 spoon haldi (aadha spoon)
  • 1 spoon namak (full bhar ke)
  • 1 spoon hari mirch ka paste
  • 1 spoon adrak lehsan ka paste
  • 8 adad imli ke dany

Ap ne masala tayyar kar liya. Ab ap is masaly ko pateli mein daalein aur 75ml oil shamil karein.
1 cup pani shamil karein, 3 adad tamatar bareek cut kar ke medium size tamatar shamil karein.

Ab pateli ko chulhay par rakhein, aag jala lein aur 12 minute tak is masaly ko achi tarah bhonay karein.
Pani khushk karna hai, tamatar mukammal machna hai, tail upar ana chahiye.

Ab is mein 500 gram (aadha kilo) aloo shamil karein. 1 aloo ke 2 piece honay chahiye. Aloo ko 5 minute tak bhonay karein.

Ab is mein 2 cup pani shamil karein aur pateli par dhakkan laga kar halki aanch par 6 minute tak pakayein takay aloo 50% gal jayein.

6 minute aloo pakane ke baad is mein 1kg chicken shamil karna hai lekin isay hilana ya mix nahi karna. Chulhay ko bilkul halki aanch kar dein.

Ab 125 gram dahi (aadha pao) lein, us mein 1 spoon biryani ka masala shamil karein. Ye masala banane ka tareeqa website par mojud hai.
Yehi secret masala hai jo Karachi ke mashoor bakwan centre par use hota hai lekin bataya nahi jata. Ap wahan se dekh kar masala bana sakte hain.

Ab is mein 1/2 spoon food colour (aadha spoon) shamil karein. Ab dahi ko achi tarah mix karein.

Ab ye dahi wala masala us chicken par dalna hai jise hum ne mix nahi kiya tha.
Dahi masala shamil karne ke baad hum mix karein ge aur 5 se 7 minute tak chicken ko pakayein ge takay chicken bhi 50% pak jaye.

Ab hum chulha band kar dein ge aur is ke upar dhaniya podina bareek cut kar ke phela dein. 10 adad hari mirch sabit phela kar dal dein.

Rice ke liye:

Pani garam karein, us mein 3 spoon namak full bhar kar daalein. Pani ke boil hone ka wait karein. Jab pani boil ho jaye to us mein rice shamil karein aur sath hilate bhi rahein.

Yahan ap ko koi aur kaam nahi karna, mukammal tawaja rice par rakhni hai. Rice ko dekhte rahein, utha kar torr lein:

  • Pehli martaba chawal 3 hisson mein tutay ga
  • Dusri bar 2 hisson mein tutay ga
  • Teesri martaba chawal chak karein ge to tutay ga nahi, balki halka sa dab jayega (yani 80% rice rakhne hain)

Phir chulha band kar dena hai aur rice chan lein. Pani nikaal dein aur pateli mein dal kar achi tarah phela lein.

Is ke baad 75ml oil upar se phela kar dal dena hai. Ab dhakkan band kar dein.
Upar koi bhari wazan dal kar rakh dein aur chulha jala kar full kar dein. Jab pateli ke dhakkan ki taraf se bhaap nikalay to chulhay ko bilkul low kar dein.
Is tarah 15 minute dum par lagayein. 15 minute baad ap ke Karachi ke sab se mashoor khushbodar biryani tayyar hai!

🔸 5kg Biryani

Yaad rakhein: 5kg biryani mein 70 gram mirchi lagti hai. Beshak peesi hui ya hari mirchi ka paste sab ko mix kar ke hi daalein, lekin sab ko jama karne par 70 gram honi chahiye.

Jab bhi zyada biryani banayein, zeera hamesha 2 spoon chicken ke level se kam rakhna hai.

  • Agar 5kg chicken shamil kar rahe hain to 3 table spoon zeera jayega
  • Agar 10kg chicken dal rahe hain to 7 table spoon zeera jayega
  • Mirchi 150 gram dalni hai (peesi hui ya hari mirchi ka paste sab mila kar total 150 gram)

Agar ye samajh nahi aa raha to jab bhi biryani ka qorma banayein, chak kar lein. Mirchi aur namak halka tez hona chahiye.

Agar imli ko skip karna hai to aloo bukhara shamil karein, sath mein lemon bareek gol gol cut kar ke shamil karein.
Agar zyada lemon shamil karain ge to biryani karwi ho jayegi.

⚠️ Ehtiyat:

Badiyan ke phool, dar cheeni, long — ye hamesha limit mein use karein. Beshak kuch kam ho jayein lekin zyada na hon, biryani kharab ya karwi ho sakti hai.

Jab ap biryani ka spoon khayein to biryani mun mein ja kar khushk na rahe, balki juicy ho, chatpati ho.

Main ne apni taraf se har mumkin tareeqay se samjha diya hai. 1 se 2 bar practice karne se ap kamyab ho jayein ge.
