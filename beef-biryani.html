🥩 Karachi Beef Biryani

Asslam Alikum

Aj hum ap ko bohat he lazeez Beef Biryani banana seekha rahe hain — jo tasty aur juicy ho gi!

🔸 Ingredients (اجزاء):

  • 1kg rice Kainat 1121 (1 ghanty ke liye pani mein bhego kar rakhein)
  • 1kg beef choti boti

🔸 Beef ko tender karne ka tareeqa:

Pateli mein 1kg beef daalein aur ye sab masalajat shamil karein:

  • 1 table spoon zeera
  • 1 tea spoon long
  • 1 tea spoon kali mirch
  • 7 adad badiyan ke phool
  • 5 adad dar cheeni ke tukray
  • 2 table spoon adrak lehsan ka paste
  • 1.5 table spoon namak
  • 1 table spoon lal mirch powder
  • 1 tea spoon kuti mirch
  • 1 table spoon haldi
  • 250 gram tamatar (cut kare hue)
  • 1 table spoon hari mirch ka paste
  • 100 gram oil (ya 3 spoon oil)
  • 750 ml pani

🔸 Step 1: Beef ko dum par lagayein

  • Pateli mein beef aur ye sab masalayat shamil karein
  • 1 liter se kam pani shamil karein
  • Pateli ke neeche chulha jala lein
  • Jab pani boil honey lage to chulhy ko low kar dein
  • Dum par laga dein, dhakkan ke upar koi wazan rakh dein
  • 45 minute tak dum par lagayein

🔸 Step 2: Beef check karein aur aloo shamil karein

  • Jab 45 minute dum par ho jayein to dhakkan hatayein
  • Beef ko chak karein — boti bilkul naram honi chahiye
  • Ab is mein 750 gram aloo shamil karein (1 aloo ke 2 tukray)
  • 💡 Ap chahein to aloo skip bhi kar sakte hain
  • Aloo shamil karne ke baad mix karein aur dhakkan laga dein
  • Isi dum par 10 minute lagaayein — aloo 70% gal jayein
  • Ab chulha band kar dein ya bilkul 0 par kar dein

🔸 Step 3: Dahi ka masala tayyar karein

  • 250 gram dahi mein 1 table spoon biryani masala shamil karein (biryani masaly ki recipe website par mojud hai)
  • 1/2 spoon food colour shamil karein
  • Dahi mein achi tarah mix kar ke pateli mein shamil karein
  • Mix karein — taake aloo bhi colour pakar lein

🔸 Step 4: Masala tayyar hai

  • 10 adad aloo bukhara shamil karein
  • 1/2 dhaniya, 1/2 podina (bareek cut) chaaron taraf phela dein
  • 100 gram brown piyaz chaaron taraf phela dein
  • 10 adad hari mirch chaaron taraf phela dein

🔸 Step 5: Rice boil karein

  • Rice ko 80% boil karein
  • Pani se nikaal kar pateli mein phela dein
  • 💡 Rice dalne se pehle chak kar lein — 1/2 liter yakhni honi chahiye

🔸 Step 6: Dum dein

  • Rice ke upar chaaron taraf 150 gram oil achi tarah phela lein
  • Dhakkan band kar dein aur chulha jala lein
  • Tez aag par bhaap ane tak pakayein
  • Jab pateli ke dhakkan se bhaap nikalne lage to chulha kam kar dein
  • Low aag par dum par laga dein — 10 minute tak

✅ Ap ke Karachi ke mashoor aur mazaydar Beef Biryani ready hai!
