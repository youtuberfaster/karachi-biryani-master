🫘 Khoy Wale Lahori Cholay (Commercial Recipe)

Asslam Alikum

Aj hum ap ko Lahori Khoy Wale Chanay banana seekha rahe hain!
✨ Ye commercial recipe hai! ✨

🔸 Ingredients (اجزاء):

  • 1kg chotay walay cholay
  • 100 gram masoor ki daal
  • 1 table spoon soda
  • 500 gram oil
  • 250 gram milk
  • 2 table spoon cream / malai

🔸 Masalayat (مصالحہ جات):

  • 2 table spoon namak (hasb-e-zaroorat)
  • 1 table spoon kuta dhaniya
  • 1 table spoon kuta zeera
  • 1 table spoon haldi
  • 1/2 tea spoon jaifal javetri (kuta hua)
  • 1 table spoon kuti kali mirch
  • 5 table spoon hari mirch ka paste
💡 Khas baat: Khoy walay cholay mein piyaz, kuti mirch, haldi nahi shamil hoti — lekin ap chahein to kuti mirch shamil kar sakte hain

🔸 Step 1: Cholay aur daal bhego dein

  • Raat ko 1kg chotay walay cholay lein
  • 100 gram masoor ki daal mix kar lein
  • 1 table spoon soda daalein
  • Itna pani daalein ke cholay aur daal doob jayein
  • Poori raat (8-10 ghanty) ke liye chod dein

🔸 Step 2: Subah cholay wash karein

  • Subah cholay mein se soda wala pani nikaal dein
  • Achi tarah wash karein — taake soday ka asar khatam ho jaye

🔸 Step 3: Cholay boil karein

  • Ab is mein itna pani daalein ke cholay aur daal achi tarah doob jayein
  • Chulha jala lein aur ubal ane ka wait karein
  • Jab ubal aajaye to jhag nikaal dein
  • Chulha low kar dein aur 1 ghanty (60 minute) ka dum laga dein
💡 Important: Dhakkan pora nahi lagana — thora sa chor dena hai — taake bhaap nikalati rahe
Agar aisa nahi karein ge to cholay ke chelky utar jayein ge!

🔸 Step 4: Cholay check karein

  • Jab cholay boil ho jayein aur daal bhi achi tarah gal kar pani mein shamil ho jaye
  • To chulha band kar dein
  • Ab is mein chamch chala lein
  • 4 table spoon cholay nikaal kar — mash karein — aur phir wapis cholay mein daal dein

🔸 Step 5: Masala tayyar karein

Ye sab masalayat ek saath mix kar lein:

  • 2 table spoon namak (hasb-e-zaroorat)
  • 1 table spoon kuta dhaniya
  • 1 table spoon kuta zeera
  • 1 table spoon haldi
  • 1/2 tea spoon jaifal javetri (kuta hua)
  • 1 table spoon kuti kali mirch
  • 5 table spoon hari mirch ka paste

🔸 Step 6: Masala fry karein

  • Pateli mein 500 gram oil shamil kar ke garam karein
  • Chulha bilkul low rakhein
  • Tamam masalay shamil kar ke fry karein
  • Halka halka pani bhi lagate rahein — taake masala jalay nahi
  • 3 minute tak fry karein

🔸 Step 7: Milk shamil karein

  • Ab is mein 250 gram milk shamil karein
  • Mix kar ke pakayein
  • Jab milk khushk ho jaye — to 1 cup aur milk shamil karein
  • Pakayein — isi tarah 20 minute pakayein
  • Zaroorat ke mutabiq milk shamil karte jayein

🔸 Step 8: Gravy ready ho jaye

  • Jab gravy garhi ho jaye aur oil upar aa jaye
  • To tamam oil nikaal lein (alag rakh lein)

🔸 Step 9: Cream aur cholay shamil karein

  • Ab is mein 2 table spoon cream / malai shamil karein aur mix karein
  • Ab boil kiye hue cholay daalein aur mix karein
  • Cholay ko boil karte waqt jo pani tha — wo bhi shamil karein

🔸 Step 10: Oil aur dum

  • Jo oil nikala tha — wo is mein shamil karein
  • Achi tarah mix karein
  • Ubal ane par — chulha low kar dein
  • 5 minute ka dum laga dein

✅ Ap ke behtreen Khoy Wale Lahori Cholay ready hain!
