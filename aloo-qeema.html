🥔 Aloo Qeema (Commercial Recipe)

Asslam Alikum

Aj hum ap ko Karachi ke mashoor Aloo Qeema banana seekha rahe hain!
✨ Ye commercial recipe hai! ✨

🔸 Ingredients (اجزاء):

  • 3kg qeema
  • 2.5 liter pani
  • 2kg piyaz (bareek cut)
  • 1kg tamatar paste
  • 1kg oil
  • 3kg aloo (bareek chotay chakor shape mein cut)
  • 250 gram dahi
  • 30 adad hari mirch (sabit)
  • 1 gathi hara dhaniya (bareek cut)

🔸 Masalayat (مصالحہ جات):

  • 4 table spoon adrak lehsan ka paste
  • 4 table spoon namak
  • 6 table spoon lal mirch powder
  • 2 table spoon haldi
  • 4 table spoon kuta dhaniya
  • 2 table spoon kuta dhaniya (baad mein)
  • 1 table spoon garam masala (kuta hua)
💡 Garam masala ke liye: Zeera aur dhaniya na ho — baki garam masaly ki har cheez shamil honi chahiye

🔸 Aloo Qeema banane ka tareeqa:

Step 1: Qeema aur pani shamil karein

  • Pateli mein 3kg qeema shamil karein
  • Is mein 2.5 liter pani shamil kar ke mix karein
  • Chulha jala lein

Step 2: Masalayat shamil karein

  • Ab is mein ye sab masalayat shamil karein:
  • 4 table spoon adrak lehsan ka paste
  • 2kg piyaz (bareek cut) — qeemy ke upar phela dein
  • 4 table spoon namak — phela dein

Step 3: Dum dein

  • 5 minute chulha tez rakhein — jab tak ubal aajaye
  • Jab ubal aajaye to chulha medium kar dein
  • Dhakkan laga dein aur dhakkan par koi wazan rakh kar 30 minute ka dum laga dein

Step 4: Chamch chalayein

  • 30 minute ke baad dhakkan hatayein
  • Achi tarah chamch chalayein — taake piyaz mukammal ghaib ho jaye

Step 5: Tamatar paste shamil karein

  • Ab is mein 1kg tamatar paste shamil karein
  • Achi tarah mix karein
💡 Note: Tamatar ko pehly boil karna hai — phir uska paste banana hai

Step 6: Lal mirch aur haldi shamil karein

  • Ab ye tamam masalayat shamil karein:
  • 6 table spoon lal mirch powder
  • 2 table spoon haldi

Step 7: Pani khushk karein aur oil daalein

  • In sab ko achi tarah mix karein
  • Pani khushk hone tak pakayein
  • 1kg oil shamil karein
  • 30 minute tak musalsal bhoonai karein

Step 8: Dhaniya powder shamil karein

  • Ab is mein 4 table spoon kuta dhaniya shamil karein
  • Achi tarah mix karein

Step 9: Aloo shamil karein

  • Jab oil mukammal upar aa jaye
  • To is mein 3kg aloo (bareek chotay chakor shape mein cut) shamil karein
  • Achi tarah mix kar ke fry karein

Step 10: Dahi aur hari mirch shamil karein

  • Jab fry karte hue oil upar aa jaye
  • To is mein 250 gram dahi shamil karein
  • 30 adad hari mirch (sabit) shamil karein
  • Achi tarah mix karein

Step 11: Dum dein

  • Ab is mein 1 glass pani shamil karein
  • Chulha low kar ke 10 minute ka dum laga dein — taake aloo gal jayein

Step 12: Dum ke baad fry karein

  • 10 minute ke baad dhakkan hatayein
  • Fry karein — oil alag ho jaye

Step 13: Dhaniya aur garam masala shamil karein

  • Ab is mein 2 table spoon kuta dhaniya shamil karein aur mix karein
  • 1 table spoon garam masala (kuta hua) shamil karein

Step 14: Garnish karein

  • Ab is mein 1 gathi hara dhaniya (bareek cut) phela dein

✅ Ap ke bohat he behtreen Aloo Qeema ready hai!
