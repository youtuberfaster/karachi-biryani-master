🌿 Chicken Green Karahi

Asslam Alikum

Aj hum ap ko Karachi ke mashoor Chicken Green Karahi banana seekha rahe hain!

🔸 Ingredients (اجزاء):

  • 1kg chicken
  • 250 gram dahi
  • 250 gram green paste

🌱 Green Paste banane ka tareeqa:

  • 1 gathi dhaniya
  • 1 gathi podina
  • 1 adad shimla mirch
  • 2 adad hari mirch
  • 2 adad palak ke pattay

Sab ka paste bana lein

🔸 Chicken Green Karahi banane ka tareeqa:

Step 1: Chicken brown karein

  • Karahi mein oil shamil karein aur garam karein
  • Is mein 1kg chicken shamil karein
  • Achi tarah brown karein

Step 2: Extra oil nikaal lein

  • Chicken brown hone ke baad falto oil nikaal lein
  • 200 gram ke barabar oil chor dein

Step 3: Adrak lehsan shamil karein

  • Ab is mein 2 table spoon adrak lehsan ka paste shamil karein
  • Achi tarah mix karein
  • Chulha kam rakhein

Step 4: Green paste shamil karein

  • Ab is mein green paste shamil karein
  • Achi tarah fry karein
  • 2 minute ka dum bhi laga dein

Step 5: Masalayat shamil karein

  • 1 table spoon kali mirch (kuti hui)
  • 1 table spoon dhaniya (kuta hua)
  • 1 table spoon zeera (kuta hua)

Step 6: Bhoonai karein

  • In sab ko achi tarah mix karein
  • Taake masalay aur green paste achi tarah bhoonai ho jaye

Step 7: Dahi shamil karein

  • Ab is mein 250 gram dahi shamil karein
  • Achi tarah mix karein
  • 2 minute fry karne ke baad chulha band kar dein

Step 8: Garnish karein

  • Karahi mein adrak (lambi katti hui) phela dein
  • 4 tukray lemon (gol bareek cut kiye hue) phela dein

✅ Ap ke Karachi ke bohat mazaydar Chicken Green Karahi ready hai!
