🍛 Karachi Banu Pulao (Nali Pulao)

Asslam Alikum

Aj hum ap ko Karachi ka mashoor aur bohat he mazaydar Banu Pulao banana seekha rahe hain!

🔸 Ingredients (اجزاء):

  • 2kg rice Super Karnal Banspati (40 minute pani mein bhegona lazmi hai)
  • 2kg beef choti boti + 2 daad nali (ye zaroori hai)

🔸 Banu Pulao banane ka tareeqa:

Step 1: Oil aur piyaz

  • Pateli mein 100 gram oil daalein aur garam karein
  • 500 gram piyaz shamil karein aur brown karein

Step 2: Tamatar aur adrak lehsan

  • 500 gram tamatar (cutting kar ke) shamil karein aur mix karein
  • 4 table spoon adrak lehsan ka paste shamil karein aur achi tarah mix karein

Step 3: Beef shamil karein

  • 2kg beef + 2 nali ke sath shamil karein
  • Achi tarah mix kar ke 1/2 liter pani shamil karein
  • 3 table spoon kuti mirch shamil karein
  • 2 table spoon namak shamil karein

Step 4: Beef ki bhoonai

  • Achi tarah mix kar ke 10 minute tak achi tarah beef ki bhoonai karein
  • Pani khushk karein
  • Chulhy ki aanch medium rakhein
  • Jab oil upar aajaye aur gravy dany daar ban jaye

Step 5: Pani shamil karein

  • Ab is mein 4 liter pani shamil karein
  • Isi pani mein beef bhi galani hai aur rice bhi pakana hai
  • Chulhy ki aanch ko high karein
  • Dhakkan laga dein — taake pani mein ubal aajaye

Step 6: Beef ko dum par lagayein

  • Pani mein ubal aane ke baad chulhy ki aanch ko low kar dein
  • Dhakkan achi tarah band kar ke 40 minute dum par laga dein
  • 💡 Taake beef ki boti bilkul naram mulaim ho jaye

Step 7: Yakhni mein masalay shamil karein

Beef ke naram ho jane ke baad ap ke paas 2.5 liter yakhni bache gi:

  • 125 gram hari mirch (sabit) shamil karein
  • 125 gram aloo bukhara yakhni mein shamil karein
  • 3 table spoon garam masala (kuta hua) shamil karein
  • 3 table spoon kali mirch (kuti hui) shamil karein

Step 8: Rice shamil karein

  • Ab is mein 2kg rice shamil karein
  • Rice yakhni mein doob jayein
  • Aaram aaram se mix karein — kyunke ye kachi Banspati hoti hai, tootne ka andesha rehta hai
  • Chulha medium hona chahiye
  • Chamch khara kar ke mix karein — gol gol nahi ghumana

Step 9: Dum dein

  • Jab tamam pani khushk ho jaye — 1 cup ke barabar pani bache
  • Chulhy ko bilkul low kar dein
  • Dhakkan laga kar oper wazan rakh dein

Step 10: Final dum

  • Rice ko 20 minute ka bilkul low aanch par dum laga dein

✅ Ap ka behtreen aur Karachi ka mashoor gosht dar nali wala Banu Pulao ready hai!
