🌶️ Secret Biryani Masala

Asslam Alikum

⭐ Secret Recipe! ⭐
Aj hum ap ko biryani ka wo secret masala banana seekha rahe hain jo dahi mein shamil kar ke biryani mein phela kar dala jata hai!
📌 Karachi ke bary aur mashoor biryani ke pakwan centre nahi batate — balky secret rakhte hain!

🔸 Ingredients (اجزاء):

  • 30 gram green choti elaichi
  • 30 gram jaifal (chhilky ke sath — isay tor kar ander se jaifal nikaal lein)
  • 30 gram javetri
  • 15 gram long
  • 30 gram American badam (lambe wale)

🔸 Biryani Secret Masala banane ka tareeqa:

Step 1: Masalay roast karein

  • Sab se pehly frypan lein
  • Elaichi nahi dalni — baqi sab masalay (jaifal, javetri, long, American badam) frypan mein daalein
  • 20 second roast karein (zyada nahi — bas khushbu ane tak)

Step 2: Grand karein

  • Ab in sab ko choti elaichi ke sath achi tarah grind (pees) lein
  • Bareek powder bana lein
💡 Ye masala grind hone ke baad ready hai!
Jab bhi biryani banayein — rice ke hisaab se dahi mein food colour ke sath yehi masala shamil karein — aur dahi ko mix kar ke biryani ke chaaron taraf phela dein

🔸 Miqdaar (Quantities - Biryani ke hisaab se):

  • 1kg biryani mein — sirf aadha tea spoon dahi mein shamil karein
  • 5kg biryani mein — 1 tea spoon (bhar ke) shamil karein
  • 10kg biryani mein — 1 table spoon (bhar ke) shamil karein
✨ Nateeja: Yehi wo masala hai jo test deta hai, khushbu deta hai! ✨

✅ Ap ka behtreen Secret Biryani Masala ready hai!
