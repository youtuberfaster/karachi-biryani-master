🤍 Karachi Chicken White Biryani

Asslam Alikum

🔸 1kg Chicken White Biryani

Step 1: Pateli mein oil garam karein

Jis pateli mein white biryani banani hai, us mein 100ml oil daalein.

Step 2: Chicken fry karein

Jab oil garam ho jaye to us mein 1kg chicken shamil karein aur iska colour white hone tak fry karein.

Step 3: Masalajat shamil karein

Jab chicken ka colour change ho jaye to ye sab shamil karein:

  • 10 adad aloo bukhara shamil karein
  • 2 table spoon adrak lehsan ka paste shamil karein
  • 1 medium size tamatar (cut kiya hua) shamil karein
  • 2 tukray dar cheeni shamil karein
  • 3 adad badiyan ke phool shamil karein
  • 2 adad teez pat ke pattay shamil karein
  • 1 table spoon zeera shamil karein
  • 1 table spoon long aur kali mirch mix kar ke shamil karein
  • 5 table spoon hari mirch ka paste shamil karein
  • 50 gram dahi shamil karein
  • 1.5 spoon namak shamil karein
  • 1 gathi karhi patta shamil karein

Step 4: Bhonai karein

Ab in sab ko mix karein aur achi tarah bhonai karein. Tamatar ko mash karein.

Step 5: Sonf dhanya aur gol mirchi

  • 1 table spoon sonf aur dhanya powder mix kar ke shamil karein
  • 10 adad gol mirchi shamil karein aur mix karein

Step 6: Pani shamil karein

Phir 2 glass pani shamil karein. Pani ke ubal aate hi chulha band kar dein.

Step 7: Rice boil karein

  • Rice ko boil karein 80% tak pakana hai
  • Rice sela hona chahiye - Kainat 1121 hona chahiye

Step 8: Rice layer lagayein

  • 2 spoon dhaniya pudina mix karein aur chaaron taraf phela dein
  • Rice ko pani se achi tarah nikaal kar pateli mein daalein aur phela dein
  • 75ml oil rice ke upar phela dein

Step 9: Dum lagayein

  • Dhakkan band kar ke chulha jala lein
  • Bhaap nikalne tak tez aanch par pakayein
  • Jab bhaap nikalni start ho jaye to chulhay ko bilkul low kar dein
  • 15 minute ke liye dum par rakhein

✅ Ap ke Chicken White Biryani ready hai!
