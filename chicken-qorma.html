🍗 Karachi Chicken Qorma

Asslam Alikum

Aj hum ap ko 1kg Chicken Qorma banana seekha rahe hain — ye qorma Karachi ke mashoor pakwan center ki recipe hai!

🔸 Ingredients (اجزاء):

  • 1kg chicken (14 piece honay chahiye)
  • 750 gram dahi
  • 1kg brown piyaz (ke liye 1.5kg kacha piyaz)

🔸 Chicken Qorma banane ka tareeqa:

Step 1: Piyaz brown karein

  • Sab se pehly 1.5kg kacha piyaz (bareek cut) lein
  • Garam tel mein daal kar light brown karein
  • Piyaz brown hone ke baad use nikaal kar achi tarah phela dein — taake hawaa lagay

Step 2: Chicken brown karein

  • Pateli mein 500 gram wo oil daalein jis mein piyaz brown ki thi
  • Jab oil garam ho jaye to us mein 1kg chicken shamil karein
  • Medium aanch par chicken ko brown karein

Step 3: Adrak lehsan shamil karein

  • Jab chicken brown ho jaye to is mein 2 table spoon adrak lehsan ka paste shamil karein aur mix karein

Step 4: Dahi aur piyaz ka mix

  • Jo piyaz hum ne brown ki thi — use achi tarah kot lein
  • Us mein 500 gram dahi shamil karein aur mix karein

Step 5: Masalayat tayyar karein

Is mein ye tamam masalajat shamil karein:

  • 1.5 table spoon lal mirch powder
  • 1 table spoon haldi
  • 1 table spoon dhaniya powder
  • 1 tea spoon long
  • 1 tea spoon choti elaichi
  • 1 tea spoon kali mirch
  • Namak apne taste ke mutabiq shamil karein

Step 6: Masala chicken mein shamil karein

  • Ye tamam masaly dahi mein shamil karein
  • Phir chicken mein daal dein
  • Chulha low kar dein
  • Achi tarah bhoonai karein — halka halka pani bhi shamil karte jayein

Step 7: Pani aur dum

  • Jab achi tarah masaly ki bhoonai ho jaye to 1 liter pani shamil karein
  • Halki aanch par 5 minute ka dum laga dein — taake chicken naram ho jaye
💡 Ehteyat karein: Gravy dany daar honi chahiye aur chicken ki boti achi tarah masala jazb kare!
