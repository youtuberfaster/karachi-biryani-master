🍛 Salan Masala Banane Ka Tarika

Asslam Alikum

Aj hum ap ko aisa salan masala banana seekha rahe hain — jis ki khushbu door tak jayegi aur test bhi kafi din tak yaad rahega! ✨

🔸 Ingredients (اجزاء):

  • 2kg sabit dhaniya
  • 500 gram garam masala (mukammal sabit) — jis mein long, kali mirch, dar cheeni, jaifal, javetri, long, badiyan ke phool, teez pat ke pattay sab shamil hain
  • 250 gram sabit zeera
  • 1kg lal mirch powder
  • 1kg haldi powder
  • 1.5kg chicken powder
  • 400 gram desi lehsan (sabit)
  • 25 gram zardy ka colour
  • 20 gram food colour (red colour)

🔸 Salan Masala banane ka tareeqa:

Step 1: Tamam masalay mix karein

  • Jo masalay sabit hain aur jo powder hain — sab ko achi tarah mix karein

Step 2: Grand karein

  • Sab ko ache se grind (pees) lein
💡 Ap ka bohat he tasty Salan Masala ready hai!

🔸 Khas khyal (استعمال کرنے کا طریقہ):

  • Jab bhi salan banayein — masaly ki jagah yehi masala shamil karein
  • Mirchi aur haldi extra shamil na karein — ye masala kafi hai!
  • Sirf namak shamil karein
  • Is masaly ki achi tarah bhoonai karein
📌 Behtreen baat: Har tarhan ke salan mein is masaly ko use kiya ja sakta hai!

✅ Ap ka behtreen Salan Masala ready hai!
