🍛 Chicken Handi

Asslam Alikum

Aj hum ap ko Karachi ke mashoor Chicken Handi banana seekha rahe hain!

🔸 Ingredients (اجزاء):

  • 1kg chicken
  • 250 gram tamatar paste (ap chahein to tamatar ko 2 piece mein cut kar ke bhi shamil kar sakte hain)
  • 250 gram oil
  • 200 gram dahi
  • 100 gram blueband butter (ap desi ghee bhi use kar sakte hain)
  • 250 gram milk pack cream
  • 2 table spoon adrak lehsan ka paste

🔸 Masalayat (مصالحہ جات):

  • 1 table spoon namak
  • 1 table spoon kuti mirch
  • 1 tea spoon haldi
  • 1 table spoon kasoori methi
  • 1 tea spoon lal mirch powder
  • 1 table spoon garam masala
  • 1 table spoon kisi bhi brand ka karahi masala
  • 1 table spoon kuta dhaniya
  • 1 table spoon kali mirch powder
  • 1 table spoon zeera (kuta hua)

🔸 Chicken Handi banane ka tareeqa:

Step 1: Chicken fry karein

  • Sab se pehly karahi mein 250 gram oil shamil karein aur garam karein
  • Is mein 1kg chicken shamil karein aur 5 minute fry karein
  • 💡 Yaad rakhein: Chicken brown nahi karna hai!

Step 2: Adrak lehsan shamil karein

  • Jab ap chicken fry kar lein to is mein 2 table spoon adrak lehsan ka paste shamil karein
  • Fry karein — chulha halka rakhein

Step 3: Tamatar paste aur dum

  • Ab is mein 250 gram tamatar paste shamil karein
  • Achi tarah mix kar ke is mein 1 cup pani shamil kar ke 5 minute ka dum laga dein

Step 4: Pani khushk ho jaye

  • Jab pani khushk ho jaye aur oil upar aa jaye
  • To is mein ye masalayat shamil karein:

Step 5: Pehla batch masala

  • 1 table spoon kuti mirch
  • 1 tea spoon lal pisi mirch
  • 1 tea spoon haldi
  • 1 table spoon kasoori methi
  • 1 table spoon garam masala
  • 1 table spoon kisi bhi brand ka karahi masala

Step 6: Masala bhoonai karein

  • Masala shamil karne ke baad achi tarah mix karein
  • Taake masalon ki achi tarah bhoonai ho jaye

Step 7: Dahi shamil karein

  • Ab is mein 200 gram dahi shamil karein
  • Achi tarah mix karein
  • Chulha halka kar dein

Step 8: Dosra batch masala

  • Ab is mein ye masalayat bhi shamil karein:
  • 1 table spoon kuta dhaniya
  • 1 table spoon kuti kali mirch
  • 1 table spoon kuta zeera

Step 9: Fry karein aur cream shamil karein

  • In masalon ko bhi achi tarah mix kar ke fry karein
  • Ye masalay shamil karne ke baad chulha bilkul low kar dena hai
  • Aur 250 gram milk pack cream is mein shamil kar dein
⚠️ Ehteyat: Yad rakhein! Agar chulha teez hua aur ap ne cream shamil kar di to cream phatt jayegi aur handi kharab ho jayegi!

Step 10: Cream mix karein

  • Halke haath se cream ko achi tarah mix karein
  • Jab oil upar aa jaye to chulha band kar dein

Step 11: Handi mein serve karein

  • Ab ap ek handi lein — use chulhay par rakh kar aag full kar dena hai
  • Is mein butter shamil karna hai
  • Jab butter pighal jaye to is mein chicken handi bhar dein (karahi khali kar dein)
  • Ab handi ka chulha bhi band kar dein

Step 12: Garnish karein

  • Is ke upar adrak (lambi cut ki hui) thori si rakhein
  • 4 piece lemon (gol cut kiye hue) rakhein
  • Thora sa dhaniya bhi rakhein

✅ Ap ke Karachi ke sab se mashoor aur lazeez Chicken Handi ready hai!
