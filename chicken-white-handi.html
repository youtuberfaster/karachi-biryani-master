🤍 Chicken White Handi

Asslam Alikum

Aj hum ap ko Karachi ke bohat he mashoor Chicken White Handi banana seekha rahe hain!

🔸 Ingredients (اجزاء):

  • 1kg chicken boneless
  • 1 liter milk
  • 100 gram oil (extra oil nikaalne ke baad)
  • 2 table spoon kuta dhaniya
  • 1 slice cheddar cheese
  • 200 gram cream
  • 150 gram dahi
  • 4 table spoon desi ghee
  • 100 gram blueband butter

🔸 Masalayat (مصالحہ جات):

  • 1 table spoon zeera
  • 1.5 table spoon kali mirch (kuti hui)
  • 1.5 table spoon white mirch
  • 1 table spoon khopra powder

🔸 Chicken White Handi banane ka tareeqa:

Step 1: Chicken fry karein

  • Sab se pehly karahi mein oil garam karein
  • Is mein 1kg chicken boneless shamil karein
  • 5 minute tak fry karein (raw nahi karni)

Step 2: Extra oil nikaal lein

  • Karahi mein se extra oil nikaal dena hai
  • 100 gram oil chor dena hai

Step 3: Milk shamil karein

  • Ab is mein 1 liter milk shamil karna hai
  • Isay pakne dena hai
  • Jab aadha milk khushk ho jaye

Step 4: Dhaniya aur cheese shamil karein

  • To is mein 2 table spoon kuta dhaniya shamil karna hai
  • Aur 1 slice cheddar cheese shamil kar ke achi tarah mix karna hai

Step 5: Masalayat shamil karein

  • Ab is mein ye tamam masalayat shamil karein:
  • 1 table spoon zeera
  • 1.5 table spoon kali mirch (kuti hui)
  • 1.5 table spoon white mirch
  • 1 table spoon khopra powder

Step 6: Masala fry karein

  • Ye tamam masaly shamil kar ke achi tarah mix karein
  • 4 minute tak pakayein — taake masaly bhi achi tarah fry ho jayein
  • 💡 Note: Fry karte hue agar khushk ho jaye to thora sa milk shamil karna hai — pani ka use nahi hoga!

Step 7: Cream shamil karein

  • Masaly achi tarah fry hone ke baad chulha bilkul low kar dein
  • Aur is mein 200 gram cream shamil karein
  • Achi tarah mix karein

Step 8: Dahi aur desi ghee shamil karein

  • Ab is mein 150 gram dahi shamil karein
  • Achi tarah mix karein
  • Ab is mein 4 table spoon desi ghee shamil karein
  • Halka mix karein aur chulha band kar dein

Step 9: Handi tayyar karein

  • Ab handi (matti ki ho ya steel ki) lein
  • Us mein 100 gram blueband butter daalein
  • Chulhay par rakh kar tez garam karein — taake puri handi achi tarah garam ho jaye aur butter bhi pighal jaye

Step 10: Handi mein shift karein

  • Ab karahi se handi mein shift karna hai
  • Handi ko mukammal bhar dein
  • Jab karahi khali ho jaye aur mukammal handi bhar jaye
  • To usay achi tarah josh dein — taake butter upar aa jaye

Step 11: Garnish karein

  • Ab is mein adrak (bareek cut ki hui lambi) phela dein
  • 4 piece lemon (gol bareek cut kiye hue) phela dein
💡 Khas khyal rakhein: Agar chicken 1kg hai to handi bhi 1kg ke size ki hi leni hai!

✅ Ap ke Chicken White Handi ready hai!
