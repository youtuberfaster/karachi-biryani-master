🐏 Mutton Karahi

Asslam Alikum

Aj hum ap ko Karachi ke bohat he mashoor Mutton Karahi banana seekha rahe hain!

🔸 Ingredients (اجزاء):

  • 1kg mutton
  • 1 table spoon namak
  • 200 gram oil
  • Pani (zaroorat ke mutabiq)
  • 500 gram tamatar (1 tamatar ke 2 tukray)
  • 200 gram dahi

🔸 Masalayat (مصالحہ جات):

  • 1 table spoon lal kuti mirch
  • 1 tea spoon pisi lal mirch
  • 1/2 tea spoon haldi
  • 1 table spoon kali mirch (kuti hui)
  • 1 table spoon dhaniya powder
  • 1 table spoon zeera (kuta hua)

🔸 Mutton Karahi banane ka tareeqa:

Step 1: Mutton ko dum par lagayein

  • 1kg mutton karahi mein daalein
  • 1 table spoon namak shamil karein
  • 200 gram oil shamil karein
  • Pani shamil karein
  • Chulha jala lein — jab pani mein josh aajaye
  • To chulha low kar dein aur 45 minute ka dum laga dein

Step 2: Tamatar shamil karein

  • 45 minute dum lagane ke baad mutton naram ho gayi hogi
  • Is mein 500 gram tamatar shamil karein (1 tamatar ke 2 tukray)

Step 3: Dum dein aur tamatar mash karein

  • Tamatar shamil karne ke baad 5 minute ka dum laga dein
  • 5 minute baad tamatar ke chelky nikaal dein
  • Tamatar ko achi tarah mash karein

Step 4: Masalayat shamil karein

  • Jab oil upar aajaye to ye masalayat shamil karein:
  • 1 table spoon lal kuti mirch
  • 1 tea spoon pisi lal mirch
  • 1/2 tea spoon haldi
  • 1 table spoon kali mirch (kuti hui)
  • 1 table spoon dhaniya powder
  • 1 table spoon zeera (kuta hua)

Step 5: Masala fry karein

  • In masalon ko bhi achi tarah mix karein aur fry karein

Step 6: Dahi shamil karein

  • Ab 200 gram dahi shamil karein
  • Dahi shamil karne ke baad achi tarah mix karein aur fry karein

Step 7: Garnish karein

  • Ab chulha band kar dein
  • Adrak (lambi cut ki hui) phela dein
  • Dhaniya (bareek cut kar ke) phela dein
⚠️ Ehteyat: 1kg mutton karahi mein 500 gram tamatar kafi hai!
Zyada daalenge to mutton karahi meethi ho jayegi!
