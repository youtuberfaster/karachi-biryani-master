🐑 Dumba Karahi

Asslam Alikum

Aj hum ap ko Karachi ke sab se mashoor Dumba Karahi banana seekha rahe hain!

🔸 Ingredients (اجزاء):

  • 300 gram dumba ki charbi (choti boti kar ke)
  • 1kg dumba ka beef (choti boti kar ke)
  • 1 table spoon namak
  • Garam pani
  • 2 table spoon adrak lehsan ka paste
  • 500 gram tamatar (1 tamatar ke 2 tukray)
  • 200 gram dahi
  • 5 adad hari mirch (sabit, lambai mein 2 tukray kar ke)

🔸 Masalayat (مصالحہ جات):

  • 1 table spoon kuti aur pisi mirch (mix kar ke)
  • 1 tea spoon kali mirch
  • 1 table spoon kuta dhaniya
  • 1 table spoon kuta zeera

🔸 Dumba Karahi banane ka tareeqa:

Step 1: Charbi ka ghee nikaalein

  • 300 gram dumba ki charbi ko choti boti kar ke karahi mein daalein
  • Iska ghee nikaal lein

Step 2: Beef fry karein

  • Ab is mein 1kg dumba ka beef (choti boti) shamil karein
  • 1 table spoon namak bhi shamil karein
  • Achi tarah 7 minute tak fry karein — jab tak beef ka colour change ho jaye

Step 3: Dum dein

  • Ab is mein garam pani shamil karein
  • 40 minute ka dum laga dein

Step 4: Adrak lehsan shamil karein

  • Jab 40 minute poore ho jayein aur pani khushk ho jaye
  • To 2 table spoon adrak lehsan ka paste shamil karein
  • Achi tarah fry karein

Step 5: Tamatar shamil karein

  • Ab 500 gram tamatar shamil karein (1 tamatar ke 2 tukray)
  • Is mein 1 cup pani shamil karein
  • 10 minute ka dum laga dein

Step 6: Tamatar mash karein

  • 10 minute dum ke baad tamatar ke chelky hata dein
  • Tamatar ko achi tarah mash karein aur fry karein

Step 7: Masalayat shamil karein

  • Ab is mein ye tamam masalayat shamil karein:
  • 1 table spoon kuti aur pisi mirch (mix kar ke)
  • 1 tea spoon kali mirch
  • 1 table spoon kuta dhaniya
  • 1 table spoon kuta zeera

Step 8: Masala fry karein

  • Ye masaly shamil kar ke achi tarah fry karein
  • 💡 Yaad rakhein: Jitna acha fry karein ge, utna he acha taste ayega!

Step 9: Dahi shamil karein

  • Ab is mein 200 gram dahi shamil karein
  • Achi tarah mix kar ke fry karein

Step 10: Hari mirch shamil karein

  • Ab is mein 5 adad hari mirch (sabit) ko lambai mein 2 tukray kar ke shamil karein
  • 1 minute fry karein
  • Phir chulha band kar dein

Step 11: Garnish karein

  • Adrak (lambi cut ki hui) phela dein
  • Dhaniya (bareek cut kiya hua) phela dein

✅ Ap ke Dumba Karahi ready hai!
