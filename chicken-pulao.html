🍚 Karachi Chicken Pulao

Asslam Alikum

Aj hum ap ko 1kg Chicken Pulao ki recipe seekha rahe hain

⭐ Ye recipe Karachi ke mashoor pakwan centre ki hai. Isi tareeqay se banayenge to juicy, mazaydar, lazeez pulao banega!

🔸 Pehle tayyari:

  • 1kg rice sela Kainat 1121 ko pani mein daal kar 1 ghanty ke liye bhego dein

🔸 Step 1: Chicken ko brown karein

  • Sab se pehly karahi ya pateli mein 500 gram oil shamil karein
  • Jab oil garam ho jaye to us mein 1kg chicken shamil karein
  • Chicken ko brown karna hai
  • Jab chicken brown ho jaye to use karhai se nikaal kar alag bartan mein rakh dein

🔸 Step 2: Pulao ka masala tayyar karein

Pateli mein 200 gram oil daalein (jis mein chicken brown kiya tha)

  • 250 gram piyaz (bareek cut) shamil karein aur brown karein

Masalajat (ek plate mein pehle se bana kar rakhein):

  • 50 gram adrak lehsan ka paste
  • 250 gram tamatar (bareek cut)
  • 250 gram dahi
  • 8 adad badiyan ke phool
  • 5 tukray dar cheeni (chotay)
  • 1.5 table spoon zeera
  • 1 table spoon long
  • 1 table spoon kali mirch
  • 50 gram hari mirch ka paste
  • 10 adad aloo bukhara
  • 1.5 table spoon namak
  • 1 table spoon kuti mirch (haldi aur pisi mirchi nahi dalni)
  • 1kg aloo (1 aloo ke 2 piece)

🔸 Step 3: Masala pakayein

  • Jab piyaz brown ho jaye to tamam masalajat shamil karein
  • 1 glass pani shamil karein
  • 10 minute tak achi tarhan pakayein
  • Jab tak tamatar mukammal hal ho jayein aur dany dar gravy ban jaye
  • Oil upar aana chahiye, aloo 50% tak gal jayein

🔸 Step 4: Chicken shamil karein

  • Jo chicken pehle brown kar ke rakhi thi, usay shamil karein
  • Achi tarhan mix karein
  • Chulha halka kar dein
  • Jab chicken masala pakar le to 1 liter se thora zyada pani daalein

💡 Yaad rakhein: Pulao banane ke liye jitna rice hota hai us se thora zyada pani dalna hai — taake rice us mein doob jayein!

🔸 Step 5: Masala chak karein

  • Jab pani boil ho jaye to us mein 10 adad hari mirch (sabit) shamil karein
  • 1 table spoon sabit dhaniya shamil karein
  • 1 table spoon sonf shamil karein
  • Rice shamil karne se pehle masala chak kar lein — namak halka tez hona chahiye

🔸 Step 6: Rice shamil karein aur dum dein

  • Rice shamil karein aur dekhein ke rice pani mein achi tarhan doob gaye hain
  • Halka halka khara chamch chalate rahein — taake rice na tootein
  • Jab rice pani pee lein aur pani khatam ho jaye to chulha bilkul low kar dein
  • 15 minute ka dum laga dein

✅ Ap ka Karachi ka mashoor Chicken Pulao ready hai!
