🥩 Beef Qorma Dany Dar

Asslam Alikum

Aj hum ap ko Karachi ke sab se mashoor Dany Dar Beef Qorma banana seekha rahe hain!

🔸 Ingredients (اجزاء):

  • 750 gram dahi (phainta hua)
  • 150 gram oil
  • 1kg beef
  • 250 gram brown piyaz (achi tarah kuti hui)

🔸 Masalayat (مصالحہ جات):

  • 1 tea spoon haldi
  • 1 table spoon namak
  • 1 table spoon lal mirch powder
  • 7 adad choti elaichi (green wali)
  • 5 adad long
  • 10 adad kali mirch ke dany
  • 2 table spoon adrak lehsan ka paste
  • 1 table spoon dhaniya (kuta hua)

🔸 Beef Qorma Dany Dar banane ka tareeqa:

Step 1: Dahi aur oil shamil karein

  • Bateli mein 750 gram phainta hua dahi daalein
  • Ab is mein 150 gram oil shamil karein

Step 2: Masalayat shamil karein

  • Ab ye sab masalayat shamil karein:
  • 1 tea spoon haldi
  • 1 table spoon namak
  • 1 table spoon lal mirch powder
  • 7 adad choti elaichi (green wali)
  • 5 adad long
  • 10 adad kali mirch ke dany
  • 2 table spoon adrak lehsan ka paste
  • 1 table spoon dhaniya (kuta hua)

Step 3: Mix karein aur beef shamil karein

  • Ye sab masaly shamil kar ke achi tarah mix karein
  • Chulha jala lein
  • 1kg beef shamil karein
  • 5 se 10 minute tak pakayein

Step 4: Dum par laga dein

  • Ab beef ko dum par laga dein
  • 60 minute ke liye
💡 Yaad rakhein: Is mein pani shamil nahi hoga!
Dahi ke pani mein aur beef apna pani chode gi — usi mein beef galani hai.

Step 5: Brown piyaz shamil karein

  • 60 minute baad beef naram ho jayegi
  • Is mein 250 gram brown piyaz (achi tarah kuti hui) shamil karein
  • Mix kar ke bhoonai karein

✅ Ap ka Beef Qorma Dany Dar tayyar hai!

🥩🍛 Bohat he mazaydar!

📌 Khas hidayat: Ye khalis dany dar beef qorma hai — is mein gravy kam hoti hai.

Agar ap is ki gravy barhana chahte hain to:
• 500 gram tamatar lein — unhe beech mein 2 tukray karein
• Pani mein daal kar boil karein
• Jab tamatar boil ho jaye to thanda hone par pani se nikaal lein
• Juicer ya kisi tarah grind kar lein
• Qormay mein shamil karein
• 1 sachey Shan qorma masala bhi shamil kar dein
• Ap ka pehle se acha qorma banega aur kafi gravy bhi hogi ✨
