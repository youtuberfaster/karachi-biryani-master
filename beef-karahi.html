🥩 Beef Karahi

Asslam Alikum

Aj hum ap ko Karachi ke sab se mashoor Beef Karahi banana seekha rahe hain!

🔸 Ingredients (اجزاء):

  • 1kg beef
  • 1 table spoon namak
  • 200 gram oil
  • Garam pani (zaroorat ke mutabiq)
  • 2 table spoon adrak lehsan ka paste
  • 500 gram tamatar (1 tamatar ke 2 tukray)
  • 200 gram dahi

🔸 Masalayat (مصالحہ جات):

  • 1 table spoon kali mirch powder
  • 1 table spoon lal kuti mirch powder

🔸 Beef Karahi banane ka tareeqa:

Step 1: Beef ko dum par lagayein

  • Karahi mein 1kg beef shamil karein
  • 1 table spoon namak shamil karein
  • 200 gram oil shamil karein
  • Garam pani shamil kar ke chulha jala lein
  • 45 minute ka dum laga dein

Step 2: Beef check karein

  • 45 minute baad chak karein
  • Agar beef naram ho gayi hai to theek hai
  • Nahi to 1 glass dobarah garam pani shamil kar ke 10 minute ka dum dein

Step 3: Beef fry karein

  • Jab pani khushk ho jaye aur oil mein beef ko fry karein
  • 2 table spoon adrak lehsan ka paste shamil karein
  • Achi tarah mix karein

Step 4: Tamatar shamil karein

  • Ab is mein 500 gram tamatar shamil karein (1 tamatar ke 2 tukray)
  • 1 cup pani shamil karein
  • 7 minute ka dum laga dein

Step 5: Tamatar mash karein

  • 7 minute dum lagane ke baad tamatar ke chelky hata dein
  • Tamatar ko achi tarah mash karein

Step 6: Masalayat shamil karein

  • Ab is mein sirf ye 2 masaly shamil karein:
  • 1 table spoon kali mirch powder
  • 1 table spoon lal kuti mirch powder

Step 7: Mix karein

  • Masaly shamil kar ke achi tarah mix karein

Step 8: Dahi shamil karein

  • Ab is mein 200 gram dahi shamil karein
  • Achi tarah mix karein
⚠️ Ehteyat: Is karahi mein tamatar zyada shamil karne se karahi meethi ho jayegi!
Is liye 1kg beef mein 500 gram tamatar kafi hai.

✅ Ap ke Beef Karahi ready hai!
