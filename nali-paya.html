🦶 Nali Paya (Commercial Recipe)

Asslam Alikum

Aj hum ap ko Nali Paya banana seekha rahe hain!
✨ Ye commercial recipe hai! ✨

🔸 Step 1: Nali Paya wash karein

  • Pateli mein 2 adad bhains ke pay lein
  • Is mein 10 adad nali shamil karein
  • Itna pani shamil karein ke paya doob jaye
  • Is mein 100 gram adrak lehsan ka paste shamil karein
  • Chulha jala lein
💡 Jab tak paya ke pani mein ubal aata hai — ham paya ke liye masala bana lete hain

🔸 Paya Masala (دہی کا مسالہ):

  • 2kg dahi (bade bartan mein daalein)
  • 150 gram lal mirch powder — dahi mein shamil karein
  • 120 gram dhaniya powder — dahi mein shamil karein
  • 25 gram haldi — dahi mein shamil karein
  • 200 gram adrak lehsan ka paste — dahi mein shamil karein
  • 50 gram paya masala shamil karein
  • 10 gram kala namak shamil karein
  • 150 gram Lahori namak (hasb-e-zaiqa) shamil karein
  • 1 tea spoon podina powder (khushk) shamil karein
  • 50 gram sonf powder shamil karein
  • 1 tea spoon kalonji powder shamil karein
  • 1 tea spoon ajwain powder shamil karein
💡 Paya masala banane ka tareeqa website par mojud hai — dekh kar bana lein
  • In sab ko achi tarah mix karein
  • 1 glass pani shamil karein aur achi tarah mix kar lein

🔸 Step 2: Paya boil karein

  • Jab paya mein ubal aajaye to paya nikaal lein
  • Pani phaink dein
  • Ab fresh pani se paya ko wash karein

🔸 Step 3: Oil aur piyaz

  • Pateli mein 1.5 kg oil shamil kar ke garam karein
  • Ab is mein 750 gram piyaz (bareek cut) shamil karein
  • Jab piyaz naram ho jaye to is mein ye masalayat shamil karein:
  • 20 gram dar cheeni ke tukray
  • 10 adad teez pat ke pattay

Piyaz ko light brown karein

🔸 Step 4: Dahi wala masala shamil karein

  • Jo dahi mein masala shamil kiya tha — wo is mein daal dein
  • Chulha halka kar ke achi tarah fry karein
  • Chulha low kar dein aur chamch chalate rahein
  • Zaroorat paray to halka pani shamil karte rahein
  • Pani khushk kar lein — jab oil upar aajaye

🔸 Step 5: Paya shamil karein

  • To is mein paya shamil kar dein
  • Achi tarah mix kar ke 3 minute tak pakayein

🔸 Step 6: Pani shamil karein aur dum lagaayein

  • Ab is mein pani shamil karein
  • Itna pani hona chahiye ke paya achi tarah doob jaye aur 1 inch pani upar ho
  • Chulha teez kar dein — aur ubal ane ka wait karein
  • Jab ubal aajaye to chulha kam kar dein
  • Dhakkan laga kar dum par laga dein
⚠️ 5 ghanty (300 minute) ka dum lagana hai!
Kyunke is mein nali shamil hai

🔸 Step 7: Dum ke baad

  • Jab dum ko 5 ghanty ho jayein to dhakkan hatayein
  • Chak karein
  • 50 gram masala is mein shamil karein
💡 Garam masala: Jis mein zeera aur sabit dhaniya shamil na ho — baqi sab masaly shamil honay chahiye

✅ Bohat he lazeez Nali Paya tayyar hai!
