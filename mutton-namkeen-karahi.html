🧂 Mutton Namkeen Karahi

Asslam Alikum

Aj hum ap ko Namkeen Mutton Karahi banana seekha rahe hain!

🔸 Ingredients (اجزاء):

  • 300 gram dumba ki chaaki (choti boti karein)
  • Ghee (nekalne ke liye)
  • 2 table spoon adrak lehsan ka paste
  • Garam pani
  • 1 table spoon namak
  • 10 adad hari mirch (sabit)

🔸 Mutton Namkeen Karahi banane ka tareeqa:

Step 1: Mutton tayyar karein

  • 300 gram dumba ki chaaki lein
  • Choti boti karein
  • Use karahi mein daalein aur ghee nikaal lein

Step 2: Mutton fry karein

  • Ab is ghee mein mutton ko fry karein
  • Jab mutton ka colour change ho jaye

Step 3: Adrak lehsan shamil karein

  • To is mein 2 table spoon adrak lehsan ka paste shamil karein
  • Achi tarah mix karein

Step 4: Pani aur namak shamil karein

  • Ab is mein garam pani shamil karein
  • 1 table spoon namak shamil karein
  • 40 minute ka dum laga dein

Step 5: Fry karein aur hari mirch shamil karein

  • 40 minute dum ke baad mutton ko achi tarah fry karein
  • 10 adad hari mirch (sabit) shamil karein
  • Fry karein

✅ Ap ke Mutton Namkeen Karahi ready hai!
