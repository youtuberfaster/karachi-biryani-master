🫘 Dal Chana (Commercial Recipe)

Asslam Alikum

Aj hum ap ko Karachi ke mashoor chat patti Dal Chana banana seekha rahe hain!
✨ Ye commercial recipe hai! ✨

🔸 Ingredients (اجزاء):

  • 2kg dal chana
  • 500 gram piyaz (har piyaz ke 2 tukray)
  • 500 gram tamatar (har tamatar ke 2 tukray)
  • 500 gram oil
  • 3 table spoon adrak lehsan ka paste
  • 10 adad hari mirch (sabit)
  • 10 adad gol mirch (sabit)
  • 1 gathi hara dhaniya

🔸 Masalayat (مصالحہ جات):

  • 2 table spoon kuti lal mirch
  • 1 table spoon pisi lal mirch powder
  • 1.5 spoon haldi powder
  • 2 table spoon kuta dhaniya
  • 2 table spoon kuta zeera
  • 2 adad dar cheeni ke tukray
  • 2 adad teez pat ke pattay
  • 2 adad choti elaichi
  • 2 adad badi elaichi
  • 2 table spoon namak (hasb-e-zaroorat)
  • 2 table spoon kali mirch (kuti hui)
  • 2 table spoon garam masala
  • 2 table spoon kasoori methi (khushbu ke liye)

🔸 Dal Chana banane ka tareeqa:

Step 1: Dal ko bhego dein

  • 2kg dal chana ko raat ko pani daal kar bhego dein
  • 💡 Soda shamil nahi karna!

Step 2: Dal boil karein

  • Subha dal chana ka pani change karein
  • Fresh pani shamil kar ke boil hone par rakhein
  • Jab pani mein ubal aajaye to jhag nikaal dein
  • Dhakkan laga dein aur chulha low kar ke 35 minute ka dum laga dein
  • Jab dal 95% gal jaye to chulha band kar dein

Step 3: Piyaz aur tamatar boil karein

  • 500 gram piyaz (har piyaz ke 2 tukray) lein
  • 500 gram tamatar (har tamatar ke 2 tukray) lein
  • Donon ko pateli mein daalein
  • Itna pani shamil karein ke ye sab doob jayein
  • Achi tarah boil karein
💡 Note: Pani bilkul khushk nahi karna hai!
Boil hone ke baad chak karein — piyaz aur tamatar gulay honay chahiye

Step 4: Paste bana lein

  • Juicer ya kisi bhi tarah grind kar ke paste bana lein

Step 5: Oil garam karein aur adrak lehsan daalein

  • Pateli mein 500 gram oil shamil karein
  • Jab oil garam ho jaye to 3 table spoon adrak lehsan ka paste shamil karein aur mix karein
  • 💡 Isay zyada nahi pakana, na he brown karna hai!

Step 6: Tamatar piyaz ka paste shamil karein

  • Ab woh paste shamil karein jo hum ne tamatar aur piyaz ka banaya hai
  • Achi tarah mix karein

Step 7: Pehla batch masala shamil karein

  • Ab ye sab masalayat shamil karein:
  • 2 table spoon kuti lal mirch
  • 1 table spoon pisi lal mirch powder
  • 1.5 spoon haldi powder
  • 2 table spoon kuta dhaniya
  • 2 table spoon kuta zeera
  • 2 adad dar cheeni ke tukray
  • 2 adad teez pat ke pattay
  • 2 adad choti elaichi
  • 2 adad badi elaichi

Step 8: Masala fry karein

  • In sab ko achi tarah mix karein
  • 5 minute tak halki aanch par fry karein
  • Jab achi tarah fry ho jaye aur gravy dany daar ban jaye
  • Oil upar aajaye

Step 9: Dal shamil karein

  • Ab is mein boil ki hui dal shamil karein
  • 💡 Dal boil hone ke baad bhi us mein itna pani hona chahiye ke dal achi tarah doobi hui ho

Step 10: Dal ko pakayein

  • Chulha tez karein
  • Jab dal mein josh aajaye to hilate rahein
  • Jab ap dekhein ke dal se bubble nikal kar ap ki taraf aa rahe hain
  • To chulha bilkul low kar dein

Step 11: Dosra batch masala shamil karein

  • Ab dal mein ye masalayat shamil karein:
  • 2 table spoon namak (hasb-e-zaroorat)
  • 2 table spoon kali mirch (kuti hui)
  • 2 table spoon garam masala
  • 2 table spoon kasoori methi (khushbu ke liye)
  • 10 adad hari mirch (sabit)
  • 10 adad gol mirch (sabit)
  • 1 gathi hara dhaniya phela dein

Step 12: Dum par chod dein

  • Dal ko bilkul low aanch par chod dein
  • Jab tak tamam oil achi tarah upar aa jaye

✅ Ap ke Dal Chana ready hai!
