🧈 Chicken Makhni Handi

Asslam Alikum

Aj hum ap ko Chicken Makhni Handi banana seekha rahe hain!

🔸 Ingredients (اجزاء):

  • 1kg chicken boneless (gol boti)
  • 1 table spoon namak
  • 100 gram oil (extra oil nikaalne ke baad)
  • 250 gram tamatar paste
  • 5 spoon branded tamatar paste
  • 250 gram milk pack
  • 200 gram cream
  • 5 table spoon desi ghee (karahi ke liye)
  • 2 table spoon desi ghee (handi ke liye)

🔸 Masalayat (مصالحہ جات):

  • 1.5 table spoon kuti lal mirch
  • 1 table spoon zeera (kuta hua)
  • 1.5 spoon kali mirch (kuti hui)
  • 1 table spoon kuta dhaniya
  • 1 table spoon garam masala
  • 1 table spoon chicken powder
  • 1 table spoon khopra powder
  • 1 table spoon white mirch (kuti hui)

🔸 Chicken Makhni Handi banane ka tareeqa:

Step 1: Chicken fry karein

  • Karahi mein oil garam karein
  • Is mein 1kg chicken boneless (gol boti) shamil karein
  • 1 table spoon namak bhi shamil karein
  • 5 minute fry karein (brown nahi karna hai)

Step 2: Extra oil nikaal lein

  • 5 minute chicken fry karne ke baad extra oil nikaal dena hai
  • 100 gram oil he chor na hai
  • Chulha bilkul low kar dein

Step 3: Tamatar paste shamil karein

  • Ab is mein 250 gram tamatar paste shamil karein
  • Aur 5 spoon branded tamatar paste bhi shamil karein
  • Achi tarah mix kar ke fry karein — 2 se 3 minute tak

Step 4: Pehla batch masala shamil karein

  • Ab ye tamam masalayat shamil karein:
  • 1.5 table spoon kuti lal mirch
  • 1 table spoon zeera (kuta hua)
  • 1.5 spoon kali mirch (kuti hui)
  • 1 table spoon kuta dhaniya

Step 5: Masala fry karein

  • In sab ko achi tarah mix kar ke fry karein
  • 2 se 3 minute tak — taake masalay achi tarah fry ho jayein

Step 6: Milk pack shamil karein

  • Ab is mein 250 gram milk pack shamil karein
  • Achi tarah mix karein

Step 7: Garam masala shamil karein

  • Phir is mein 1 table spoon garam masala shamil karein
  • Achi tarah mix karein

Step 8: Cream shamil karein

  • Ab is mein 200 gram cream shamil karein
  • Chulha bilkul low kar dein
  • Cream ko achi tarah mix karein

Step 9: Dosra batch masala shamil karein

  • 1 table spoon chicken powder shamil karein
  • 1 table spoon khopra powder shamil karein
  • 1 table spoon white mirch (kuti hui) shamil karein
  • Achi tarah mix karein

Step 10: Desi ghee shamil karein

  • Ab is mein 5 table spoon desi ghee shamil karein
  • Mix karein

Step 11: Handi mein shift karein

  • Ab matti ya steel ki handi ko chulhay par rakhein
  • Is mein 2 table spoon desi ghee shamil kar ke achi tarah handi ko garam karna hai
  • Phir karahi se nikaal kar handi mein shift karein
  • Halka sa josh ane dein

✅ Ap ke Chicken Makhni Handi ready hai!
