🫘 Dal Mong (Commercial Recipe)

Asslam Alikum

Aj hum ap ko Karachi ke sab se mashoor Dal Mong banana seekha rahe hain!
✨ Ye commercial recipe hai! ✨

🔸 Ingredients (اجزاء):

  • 2.5 kg dal mong
  • 500 gram oil (pehle batch ke liye)
  • 500 gram piyaz (bareek cut)
  • 6 table spoon adrak lehsan ka paste
  • 750 gram tamatar
  • 2.5 liter pani
  • 20 adad hari mirch (sabit)
  • 1 gathi hara dhaniya (bareek cut)

🔸 Tarka (تڑکہ) ke liye:

  • 500 gram oil
  • 2 medium size piyaz (bareek cut)
  • 2 table spoon adrak lehsan ka paste
  • 2 table spoon zeera
  • 1 table spoon kuti lal mirch
  • 15 adad gol mirch

🔸 Masalayat (مصالحہ جات):

  • 10 adad long (sabit)
  • 6 adad dar cheeni ke chotay tukray
  • 1 badi elaichi
  • 2 table spoon namak (hasb-e-zaroorat)
  • 2 table spoon lal mirch powder
  • 4 table spoon kuti lal mirch
  • 2 table spoon haldi powder
  • 4 table spoon kuta dhaniya
  • 4 table spoon garam masala powder
  • 2 table spoon kasoori methi

🔸 Dal Mong banane ka tareeqa:

Step 1: Dal ko bhego dein

  • 2.5 kg dal mong ko 1 ghanty ke liye pani mein bhego dein

Step 2: Piyaz brown karein aur masalay daalein

  • Pateli mein 500 gram oil daal kar garam karein
  • Is mein 500 gram piyaz (bareek cut) shamil karein aur brown karein
  • Brown hone se kuch waqt pehly ye masalay shamil karein:
  • 10 adad long (sabit)
  • 6 adad dar cheeni ke chotay tukray
  • 1 badi elaichi shamil karein
💡 Inhein bhi achi tarah mix karein — taake piyaz brown hone tak ye bhi achi tarah fry ho jayein aur khushbu chor dein

Step 3: Adrak lehsan shamil karein

  • 6 table spoon adrak lehsan ka paste shamil karein
  • Achi tarah mix karein
  • 💡 Adrak lehsan ko halka brown karna hai

Step 4: Tamatar shamil karein

  • 750 gram tamatar shamil karein
  • Achi tarah mix kar ke fry karein
  • Tamatar mash karein

Step 5: Masalayat shamil karein

  • Ab is mein ye masalay shamil karein:
  • 2 table spoon namak (hasb-e-zaroorat)
  • 2 table spoon lal mirch powder
  • 4 table spoon kuti lal mirch
  • 2 table spoon haldi powder
  • 4 table spoon kuta dhaniya

Step 6: Masala fry karein

  • In sab ko achi tarah mix kar ke fry karein
  • Chulha halka rakhein
  • Hasb-e-zaroorat mamooli pani shamil karte rahein — pani khushk hota rahe, mamooli pani daal kar phir fry karte rahein
  • 15 minute tak aise hi fry karte rahein
  • Jab achi tarah fry ho jayein aur gravy dany daar ban jaye

Step 7: Dal shamil karein

  • Is mein 2.5 kg dal mong shamil karein
  • Achi tarah mix kar ke 25 minute fry karein — jab tak oil nikal aaye

Step 8: Pani shamil karein

  • Ab is mein 2.5 liter pani shamil karein
  • Achi tarah mix karein
  • Boil hone ka wait karein — jab ubal aajaye to jhag khatam hone tak achi tarah pakayein

Step 9: Dhakkan laga dein

  • Is par dhakkan laga dein aur 10 minute ke liye chod dein
  • 10 minute baad dhakkan hatayein aur chamch chalate rahein — jhag khatam hona chahiye

Step 10: Garam masala aur dum

  • Ab is mein 4 table spoon garam masala powder shamil karein
  • Achi tarah mix karein aur 5 minute ka dum laga dein
💡 Dal mukammal gal chuki hogi — lekin dal ka dana khatam nahi karna hai!
Ye sabit rahega — Dal Mash ki tarah.

Step 11: Kasoori methi shamil karein

  • Is par 2 table spoon kasoori methi phela dein

Step 12: Tarka lagaayein

  • 500 gram oil karahi mein daal kar garam karein
  • 2 medium size piyaz (bareek cut) shamil karein aur brown karein
  • Is mein 2 table spoon adrak lehsan ka paste shamil karein
  • 2 table spoon zeera shamil karein
  • 1 table spoon kuti lal mirch shamil karein
  • 15 adad gol mirch shamil karein
  • Ye tarka dal mong ke upar daal dein aur jald se dhakkan band kar dein

Step 13: Final garnish

  • 2 minute baad dhakkan hatayein
  • Is mein 20 adad hari mirch (sabit) shamil karein
  • 1 gathi hara dhaniya (bareek cut) shamil karein aur phela dein
📌 Khas hidayat: Agar ap dal mong dany daar nahi chahte to:
• Isay pehle dana khatam hone tak boil karein
• Lal mirch powder aur haldi bhi pehle shamil karein
• Jab dana khatam ho jaye to yehi tarka bana kar daal dein
