🦶 Lahori Paya (Commercial Recipe)

Asslam Alikum

Aj hum ap ko mashoor Lahori Paya banana seekha rahe hain!
✨ Ye commercial recipe hai! ✨

📌 Ye paya 3.5 kg ke liye hai

🔸 Pehle Paya wash karein:

  • Paya ko achi tarah namak ke khare pani se 3 baar wash karein
  • 💡 Namak ke pani se paya mein heek khatam ho jati hai!
📌 Khas khyal rakhein: Paya ko wash karte waqt paya ko namak lagayein aur 5 minute chod dein — is ke baad namak ke pani se wash karein — is ki heek mukammal khatam ho jayegi

🔸 Ingredients (اجزاء) - Paya:

  • 3.5 kg paya (beef/mutton)
  • 12 liter pani — mutton ke liye 8 liter pani

🔸 Masalayat (مصالحہ جات) - Paya mein daalne ke liye:

  • 350 gram piyaz (bareek cut)
  • 2 table spoon zeera
  • 2 table spoon sabit dhaniya
  • 5 dany choti elaichi (green)
  • 1 table spoon long
  • 1 table spoon kali mirch
  • 15 tukray choti dar cheeni
  • 7 adad badiyan ke phool
  • 10 adad teez pat ke pattay
  • 40 gram namak

🔸 Tarka (تڑکہ) ke liye:

  • 2 liter oil
  • 250 gram piyaz (bareek cut)
  • 2 table spoon adrak lehsan ka paste
  • 125 gram lal mirch powder
  • 75 gram haldi powder
  • 75 gram dhaniya powder
  • 75 gram sabit zeera
  • 100 gram dahi

🔸 Lahori Paya banane ka tareeqa:

Step 1: Paya aur masalay pateli mein daalein

  • Pateli mein 3.5 kg paya shamil karein
  • Is mein 12 liter pani daalein (mutton ke liye 8 liter)
  • Ab ye sab masalayat shamil karein:
  • 350 gram piyaz (bareek cut)
  • 2 table spoon zeera
  • 2 table spoon sabit dhaniya
  • 5 dany choti elaichi
  • 1 table spoon long
  • 1 table spoon kali mirch
  • 15 tukray choti dar cheeni
  • 7 adad badiyan ke phool
  • 10 adad teez pat ke pattay
  • 40 gram namak

Step 2: Pani mein josh aane tak pakayein

  • In sab masalon ko shamil kar ke mix karein
  • Chulha tez kar dein — takay pani mein josh aajaye
  • Jab paya mein josh aayega to jhag banay gi
💡 Important: Jhag nikalni nahi hai! — balkay mix karte rahein
  • Chulha medium kar dein
  • Jab jhag mukammal khatam ho jaye

Step 3: Dum par lagaayein

  • To isay dum par laga dein
  • Chulha low kar dein
  • Dhakkan laga kar oper wazan rakh dein
⚠️ Important: Jab dum par lagayein aur dhakkan band karein to chaaron taraf aata laga dein — takay bhaap na nikalay
  • 3 ghanty (180 minute) ka dum lagaana hai

Step 4: Paya check karein

  • 3 ghanty ke dum par paya achi tarah gal jayega
  • Aur paya ka shorba bhi fix rahega

Step 5: Tarka lagaayein

  • 2 liter oil alag pateli mein garam karein
  • 250 gram piyaz shamil kar ke brown karein

Step 6: Adrak lehsan shamil karein

  • Piyaz light brown hone ke baad 2 table spoon adrak lehsan ka paste shamil kar ke achi tarah mix karein

Step 7: Masalayat shamil karein

  • Ab is mein ye tamam masalayat shamil karein:
  • 125 gram lal mirch powder
  • 75 gram haldi powder
  • 75 gram dhaniya powder
  • 75 gram sabit zeera

Step 8: Dahi aur pani ka cheenta daalein

  • Ye sab masaly shamil kar ke achi tarah mix karein
  • 100 gram dahi shamil karein
  • Halka pani ka cheenta lagayein — takay masala jalay nahi
  • Achi tarah masaly ki bhoonai karein

Step 9: Tarka paya mein shamil karein

  • Ab isay utha kar paya mein shamil karein
  • Achi tarah mix karein

Step 10: Chulha band kar dein

  • Paya ka chulha low hi rehne dein
  • Jo tarka lagaya hai — use achi tarah mix karein
  • 5 minute baad chulha band kar dein

✅ Bohat he lazeez Lahori Paya ready hai! (Har tarah ki heek se pak)

📌 Sab se achi paya ki pehchan (Test):
Apni shahadat ki ungli aur angothy par paya ki gravy lagayein — aur inko apas mein mila kar band karein — kholne par chepka hua mehsoos hona chahiye
⚠️ Ehteyat (احتیاط):
Kuch log gravy ko garha rakhne ke liye atay ka ghol shamil karte hain — Karachi mein 90% log atay ka ghol shamil karte hain — aisa na karein!
Paya ko badnaam na karein! — Imaandaari aur mehnat par tawajah karein!
