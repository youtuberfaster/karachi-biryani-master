🫘 Dal Mash (Commercial Recipe)

Asslam Alikum

Aj hum ap ko Karachi ke mashoor Dal Mash banana seekha rahe hain!
✨ Ye commercial recipe hai! ✨

🔸 Ingredients (اجزاء):

  • 2kg dal mash
  • 750 gram oil
  • 750 gram piyaz (bareek cut)
  • 3 table spoon adrak lehsan ka paste
  • 750 gram tamatar
  • 10 adad hari mirch (sabit)
  • 1 gathi hara dhaniya (bareek cut)

🔸 Masalayat (مصالحہ جات):

  • 5 tukray dar cheeni
  • 1 tea spoon kali mirch (sabit)
  • 1 tea spoon long (sabit)
  • 2 table spoon namak (hasb-e-zaroorat)
  • 4 table spoon kuti lal mirch
  • 2 table spoon kuta dhaniya

🔸 Dal Mash banane ka tareeqa:

Step 1: Dal ko bhego dein

  • 2kg dal mash ko achi tarah wash karein
  • 4 ghanty ke liye bhego kar rakhein

Step 2: Piyaz brown karein

  • Pateli mein 750 gram oil daal kar garam karein
  • 750 gram piyaz (bareek cut) shamil karein aur brown karein

Step 3: Adrak lehsan shamil karein

  • Piyaz brown hone ke baad is mein 3 table spoon adrak lehsan ka paste shamil karein
  • Achi tarah mix karein
  • 💡 Note: Isay brown nahi karna hai — sirf khushbu ane tak pakana hai

Step 4: Tamatar shamil karein

  • 750 gram tamatar shamil karein
  • Achi tarah mix kar ke pakayein
  • Tamatar ko achi tarah mash karein

Step 5: Masalayat shamil karein

  • Ab is mein ye masalayat shamil karein:
  • 5 tukray dar cheeni
  • 1 tea spoon kali mirch (sabit)
  • 1 tea spoon long (sabit)
  • 2 table spoon namak (hasb-e-zaroorat)
  • 4 table spoon kuti lal mirch
  • 2 table spoon kuta dhaniya
💡 Important: Ye sab masaly shamil karte waqt sath sath chamch chalate rahein, mix karte rahein
Chulha kam rakhein — zaroorat parne par halka pani daal kar bhoonai karte rahein

Step 6: Masala fry karein

  • Halka pani daalte rahein aur masalon ko fry karte rahein
  • 5 minute tak achi bhoonai karein
  • Jab achi tarah khushbu ane lage aur gravy dany daar ban jaye

Step 7: Dal shamil karein

  • Tab ap is mein 2kg dal mash shamil karein
  • Halki aanch par achi tarah mix karein
  • 20 minute tak bhoonai karein

Step 8: Dum dein

  • Jab dal ki achi tarah bhoonai ho jaye aur oil nazar ane lage
  • Aur dal mein chamak ane lage
  • To is mein aadha liter pani daal kar mix karein
  • 20 minute ka dum laga dein — chulha low kar dein

Step 9: Dum ke baad check karein

  • Jab 20 minute ka dum poori ho jaye to chak karein
  • Dal mein bohat chamak ho, colour white hona chahiye

Step 10: Garnish karein

  • Ab is mein 10 adad hari mirch (sabit) phela dein
  • 1 gathi hara dhaniya (bareek cut) phela dein

✅ Ap ke Dal Mash ready hai!
