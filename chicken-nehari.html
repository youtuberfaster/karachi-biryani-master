🍗 Chicken Nehari (Commercial Recipe)

Asslam Alikum

Aj hum ap ko Karachi ke mashoor Chicken Nehari banana seekha rahe hain!
✨ Ye commercial recipe hai! ✨

🔸 Chicken Nehari Masala (مصالحہ جات):

  • 1 table spoon zeera
  • 1 adad badiyan ka phool
  • 12 adad long
  • 1 tea spoon kali mirch
  • 2 dany sonth ke chotay tukray
  • 3 adad pepli ke dany
  • 1 table spoon sonf
  • 1 table spoon sabit dhaniya
  • 5 tukray dar cheeni ke chotay

In sab ko bareek pees lein — yani ke powder na ho, halka bareek ho

  • 1.5 table spoon lal mirch powder shamil karein
  • 1 tea spoon haldi powder shamil karein
  • 1.5 table spoon namak (hasb-e-zaroorat) shamil karein
💡 In sab ko achi tarah mix karein. Is mein 5 adad kali mirch aur 5 adad long (dono sabit) shamil kar ke mix karein.

✅ Ap ka Chicken Nehari Masala ready hai!

🔸 Ingredients (اجزاء) - Chicken Nehari:

  • 250 gram oil
  • 200 gram piyaz (bareek cut)
  • 2kg chicken (boti bari ho — choti na ho)
  • 2 table spoon adrak lehsan ka paste
  • 1.5 liter pani

Atay ka mixture (Ayza):

  • 100 gram fine ata
  • 50 gram besan
  • 1/2 liter (aadha liter) pani

Garnish ke liye:

  • Adrak (bareek lambi cut)
  • Hari mirch (cut ki hui)
  • Aadha lemon (cut kar ke)
  • 1 tea spoon garam masala (jaifal javetri ke sath)

🔸 Chicken Nehari banane ka tareeqa:

Step 1: Piyaz brown karein

  • Pateli ya deeg mein 250 gram oil daal kar garam karein
  • Is mein 200 gram piyaz (bareek cut) shamil kar ke brown karein

Step 2: Chicken fry karein

  • Piyaz brown hone ke baad is mein 2kg chicken shamil karein
  • Achi tarah mix karein
  • 2 table spoon adrak lehsan ka paste shamil kar ke achi tarah mix kar ke fry karein
  • Chicken ko achi tarah fry karna hai — brown colour ane tak

Step 3: Nehari masala shamil karein

  • Ab is mein pora nehari masala shamil karein (jo hum ne banaya tha)
  • Ye masala 2kg chicken nehari ke liye fix hai
💡 Important: Is ki achi tarah bhoonai karte jayein — halka halka pani bhi daalte rahein taake masala jalay nahi

Step 4: Gravy banayein

  • Jab masala achi tarah bhoon jaye aur dany daar gravy ban jaye — piyaz bhi phat jaye
  • To is mein 1.5 liter pani shamil kar ke mix karein
  • Dhakkan laga dein — taake pani mein ubal aajaye

Step 5: Atay ka mixture (Ayza) tayyar karein

  • Ek bartan mein 100 gram fine ata daalein
  • 50 gram besan shamil karein aur mix karein
  • 1/2 liter (aadha liter) pani shamil kar ke achi tarah mix karein — taake koi ghotli na rahe

Step 6: Mixture shamil karein

  • Ab pateli ka dhakkan hatayein (pani mein ubal aa chuka hoga)
  • Is mein mixture thora thora shamil karte jayein — chamch chalate jayein
  • Jab mixture mukammal shamil ho jaye to chamch chalate rahein
  • Chulha tez rakhein — upar jhag aayegi to use nikalte rahein
  • Chamch musalsal chalate rahein

Step 7: Dum lagaayein

  • Jab ap dekhein ke laasdaar gravy ho gayi hai aur garhi bhi ho gayi hai
  • To chulha bilkul low kar dein
  • 1 tea spoon garam masala (jaifal javetri ke sath) upar phela dein
  • 15 minute ka dum laga dein

Step 8: Dum ke baad check karein

  • 15 minute ke baad dhakkan hatayein aur dekhein
  • Ap ne jitna oil dala hai — sab upar aa chuka hai
⚠️ Ehteyat (احتیاط):
• Ye nehari masala beef nehari mein shamil nahi hoga!
• Gravy apne hisaab se rakhein — na zyada garhi, na patli

Step 9: Garnish karein

  • Nehari ko plate mein daalein
  • Upar bareek lambi cut ki hui adrak phela dein
  • Hari mirch (cut ki hui) phela dein
  • Aadha lemon cut kar ke rakh dein

✅ Ap ke bohat he lazeez Chicken Nehari ready hai!
