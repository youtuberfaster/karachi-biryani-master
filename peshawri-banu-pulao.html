🥘 Peshawri Banu Pulao

Asslam Alikum

Aj hum ap ko Peshawri Banu Pulao seekha rahe hain — is mein masalajat ke sath kishmish bhi shamil hoti hai!

🔸 Ingredients (اجزاء):

  • 1kg rice Super Karnal Banspati (1 ghanty tak pani mein bhego kar rakhein)
  • 1kg beef + extra 4 adad nali

🔸 Peshawri Banu Pulao banane ka tareeqa:

Step 1: Oil aur piyaz

  • Pateli mein 100 gram oil daalein aur garam karein
  • 250 gram piyaz (bareek cut) shamil karein aur brown karein

Step 2: Adrak lehsan aur tamatar

  • 2 table spoon adrak lehsan ka paste shamil kar ke mix karein
  • 300 gram chotay tamatar lein — 2 tukron mein cut karein (1 tamatar ke 2 tukray)
💡 Important: Chulhy ki halki aanch honi chahiye aur lakri ka chamch use karna hai
  • 1 cup pani shamil kar ke dhakkan laga dein
  • 5 minute ka dum laga dein
  • 5 minute baad dhakkan hatayein
  • Tamatar ke chelkay nikaal lein
  • Tamatar ko achi tarah mash karein

Step 3: Masalayat shamil karein

Ab ye tamam masaljat pateli mein daalein:

  • 2 table spoon namak
  • 2 table spoon kuti mirch
  • 1 table spoon dhaniya powder

Step 4: Masaly ki bhoonai

  • Is masaly ki bhoonai karein
  • Tamam pani khushk karein
  • Oil upar aajaye

Step 5: Beef shamil karein

  • Ab is mein 1kg beef + 4 adad nali daalein
  • Achi tarah mix karein — beef apna pani chode gi
  • Agar zaroorat ho to mamooli sa pani shamil karein
  • Beef ki achi tarah bhoonai karein — jab tak beef ka colour change na ho jaye

Step 6: Pani shamil karein aur dum dein

  • Ab is mein 2 liter pani shamil karein
  • Chulhy ko tez kar dein — taake pani mein ubal aajaye
  • Jab pani mein ubal aajaye to chulha bilkul low kar dein
  • Dhakkan band kar ke oper wazan rakh dein
  • 40 minute ka dum laga dein
💡 Jab beef achi tarah gal jaye — boti bilkul naram ho jaye

Step 7: Zeera, kali mirch aur kishmish

  • 1 spoon zeera shamil karein
  • 1 spoon kali mirch powder shamil karein
  • 2 spoon kishmish shamil karein

Step 8: Rice shamil karein aur dum dein

  • Ab is mein 1kg rice shamil karein
  • Achi tarah mix karein
  • Namak chak kar lein
  • Jab tamam pani khushk ho jaye — 1 cup pani bache
  • Chulha bilkul low kar dein
  • 15 minute ka dum laga dein

✅ Ap ka behtreen mazaydar Peshawri Banu Pulao ready hai!
