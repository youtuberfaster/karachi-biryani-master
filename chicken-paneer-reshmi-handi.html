🧀 Chicken Paneer Reshmi Handi

Asslam Alikum

Aj hum ap ko Chicken Paneer Reshmi Handi banana seekha rahe hain!

🔸 Ingredients (اجزاء):

  • 1kg chicken boneless (finger size mein cut karein)
  • 1 table spoon namak
  • 100 gram oil (extra oil nikaalne ke baad)
  • 1.2 liter milk
  • 1 slice cheddar cheese
  • 10 tukray shimla mirch (lambe cut kiye hue)
  • 200 gram milk pack cream
  • 5 spoon desi ghee
  • 2 spoon desi ghee (handi ke liye)
  • Paneer ka 1 tukra

🔸 Masalayat (مصالحہ جات):

  • 1 table spoon zeera (kuta hua)
  • 1 table spoon kuta dhaniya
  • 1 table spoon kali mirch (kuti hui)
  • 1 table spoon white mirch (kuti hui)
  • 1 table spoon chicken powder
  • 2 table spoon khopra powder

🔸 Chicken Paneer Reshmi Handi banane ka tareeqa:

Step 1: Chicken fry karein

  • Karahi mein oil garam karein
  • 1kg chicken boneless (finger size mein cut kiya hua) shamil karein
  • 1 table spoon namak bhi sath shamil karein
  • Chicken ko 5 minute fry karna hai (brown nahi karna)

Step 2: Extra oil nikaal lein

  • 5 minute fry karne ke baad falto oil nikaal dein
  • Sirf 100 gram oil chor dena hai
  • Chulha bilkul low kar dena hai

Step 3: Masalayat shamil karein (Pehla batch)

  • Ab is mein ye masalayat shamil karein:
  • 1 table spoon zeera (kuta hua)
  • 1 table spoon kuta dhaniya
  • 1 table spoon kali mirch (kuti hui)

Step 4: Milk, cheese aur shimla mirch shamil karein

  • 1.2 liter milk shamil karein
  • 1 slice cheddar cheese shamil karein
  • 10 tukray shimla mirch (lambe cut kiye hue) shamil karein

Step 5: Mix karein aur pakayein

  • In sab ko achi tarah mix karein
  • Chulha medium kar dein
  • 2 minute tak fry karein
  • Agar khushk hone lage to thora sa milk shamil kar ke achi tarah pakayein

Step 6: Cream shamil karein

  • Ab is mein 200 gram milk pack cream shamil karein
  • Achi tarah mix kar ke fry karein

Step 7: Dosra batch masala shamil karein

  • Ab is mein ye masalayat shamil karein:
  • 1 table spoon white mirch (kuti hui)
  • 1 table spoon chicken powder
  • 2 table spoon khopra powder

Step 8: Fry karein

  • Achi tarah mix kar ke fry karna hai
  • Agar ap ko lage ke khushk ho rahi hai to mamooli sa milk shamil karein
  • Achi tarah fry karein
  • Phir chulha band kar dein

Step 9: Desi ghee shamil karein

  • Ab is mein 5 spoon desi ghee shamil karein
  • 💡 Note: Mix nahi karna

Step 10: Handi mein shift karein

  • Ab handi lein — use chulhay par rakhein
  • Us mein 2 spoon desi ghee shamil kar ke achi tarah garam karein
  • Phir karahi se nikaal kar handi mein shift kar dein
  • Josh dein

Step 11: Paneer add karein

  • Ab paneer ka 1 tukra handi ke oper rakh dena hai
  • 💡 Important: Mix nahi karna

✅ Ap ke Chicken Paneer Reshmi Handi ready hai!
