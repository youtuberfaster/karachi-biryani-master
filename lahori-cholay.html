🫘 Lahori Cholay (Commercial Recipe)

Asslam Alikum

Aj hum ap ko Lahori Cholay banana seekha rahe hain!
✨ Ye commercial recipe hai! ✨

🔸 Ingredients (اجزاء):

  • 2kg kabuli chana
  • 4 liter pani (bhegone ke liye)
  • 2 table spoon metha soda
  • 2.5 liter pani (boil karne ke liye)

🔸 Pest (پیسٹ) ke liye:

  • 250 gram piyaz
  • 50 gram hari mirch
  • 50 gram adrak lehsan

🔸 Masalayat (مصالحہ جات):

  • 750 gram oil
  • 250 gram dahi
  • 2 table spoon namak (hasb-e-zaroorat)
  • 1 table spoon kuta dhaniya
  • 1 table spoon kuta zeera
  • 4 table spoon kuti lal mirch
  • 2 table spoon haldi
  • 1.5 spoon mix garam masala (jis mein dhaniya aur zeera na ho)
  • 1.5 table spoon kali mirch (kuti hui)

🔸 Optional (Test barhane ke liye):

💡 Khas baat: Cholay boil karte waqt agar 100 gram mong masoor ki daal bhi shamil kar lein — to zyada behtar test aayega!

🔸 Step 1: Cholay bhego dein

  • 2kg kabuli chana lein
  • Is mein 4 liter pani shamil karein
  • 2 table spoon metha soda laga kar poori raat (8-10 ghanty) ke liye chod dein

🔸 Step 2: Subah cholay wash karein

  • Subah cholay ka pani geera dein
  • Chanay ko achi tarah wash karein — taake soday ka asar khatam ho jaye

🔸 Step 3: Cholay boil karein

  • Is mein 2.5 liter pani shamil karein
  • Chulha teez kar dein — taake ubal aajaye
💡 Important: Ubal ane ke baad chanay ko dum par laga dein — chulha low kar dein
Lekin dhakkan pora nahi lagana hai — thora sa chor dena hai — taake bhaap nikalati rahe
Agar aisa nahi karein ge to cholay boil hone ke waqt chelka alag hota jayega!

🔸 Step 4: Pest tayyar karein

  • 250 gram piyaz
  • 50 gram hari mirch
  • 50 gram adrak lehsan

Sab ko ek saath grind karein aur pest bana lein (cut piyaz bhi shamil kar sakte hain)

🔸 Step 5: Pest fry karein

  • Pateli mein 750 gram oil daal kar garam karein
  • Is pest ko garam oil mein shamil karein
  • 10 minute tak fry karein — lekin colour change na ho

🔸 Step 6: Dahi shamil karein

  • 250 gram dahi shamil karein
  • Achi tarah mix karein
  • 5 minute fry karein

🔸 Step 7: Masalayat shamil karein

  • Jab gravy dany daar ho jaye aur oil alag ho jaye — to ye masalay shamil karein:
  • 2 table spoon namak (hasb-e-zaroorat)
  • 1 table spoon kuta dhaniya
  • 1 table spoon kuta zeera
  • 4 table spoon kuti lal mirch
  • 2 table spoon haldi
💡 In masalon ko achi tarah mix karein — aur halka halka pani lagate jayein, fry karte jayein — taake masalay jalay nahi

🔸 Step 8: Cholay shamil karein

  • Ab is mein boil kiye hue cholay shamil karein
💡 Important: Cholay mein jo boil pani hai — wo phenkna nahi hai — balkay use bhi shamil karna hai!
  • Isay achi tarah mix karein — taake masalay aur cholay achi tarah mix ho jayein

🔸 Step 9: Garam masala aur kali mirch shamil karein

  • Ab is mein 1.5 spoon mix garam masala shamil karein (jis mein dhaniya aur zeera na ho)
  • 1.5 table spoon kali mirch (kuti hui) shamil karein
  • Achi tarah mix karein

🔸 Step 10: Dum lagaayein

  • Chulha teez kar dein
  • Jab ubal aajaye — to chulha low kar dein
  • 5 minute ka dum laga dein

✅ Bohat he lazeez nashty walay Lahori Cholay ready hain!
