🤍 Chicken White Karahi

Asslam Alikum

Aj hum ap ko Karachi ke sab se lazeez Chicken White Karahi banana seekha rahe hain!

🔸 Ingredients (اجزاء):

  • 1kg chicken
  • 1 glass pani
  • 250 gram oil
  • 1 tea spoon namak
  • 250 gram dahi
  • 5 adad hari mirch (sabit)
  • 250 gram milk pack cream

🔸 Masalayat (مصالحہ جات):

  • 1 table spoon zeera (kuta hua)
  • 1 table spoon white mirch
  • 1 table spoon chopra
  • 1 table spoon kuta dhaniya
  • 1 table spoon kali mirch (kuti hui)

🔸 Chicken White Karahi banane ka tareeqa:

Step 1: Chicken pakayein

  • 1kg chicken karahi mein daalein
  • 1 glass pani shamil karein
  • 250 gram oil shamil karein
  • Chulha jala lein aur 1 tea spoon namak shamil kar ke dhakkan laga dein

Step 2: Oil mein fry karein

  • Jab tamam pani khushk ho jaye aur oil nazar anay lage
  • 1 minute tak oil mein fry karein

Step 3: Dahi aur hari mirch shamil karein

  • Chulha kam kar dein
  • Is mein 250 gram dahi shamil karein
  • 5 adad hari mirch (sabit) shamil kar ke achi tarah mix karein

Step 4: Masalayat shamil karein

  • 1 table spoon zeera (kuta hua)
  • 1 table spoon white mirch
  • 1 table spoon chopra
  • 1 table spoon kuta dhaniya
  • 1 table spoon kali mirch (kuti hui)

Step 5: Bhoonai karein

  • In sab ko achi tarah mix karna hai
  • 2 se 3 minute ke liye — taake masalay achi tarah mix ho jayein aur bhoonai ho jaye

Step 6: Cream shamil karein

  • Ab is mein 250 gram milk pack cream shamil karein
  • Achi tarah mix karein
⚠️ Ehteyat: Jab bhi cream shamil karein, chulha bilkul kam hona chahiye — warna cream phatt jayegi aur karahi kharab ho jayegi!

Step 7: Final touch

  • Cream shamil karne ke baad jab oil upar aa jaye to chulha band kar dein
  • Adrak (lambi cut ki hui) phela dein
  • Lemon (gol cut kiya hua) phela dein

✅ Ap ke behtreen tasty Chicken White Karahi ready hai!
