🥩 Beef Nehari (Commercial Recipe)

Asslam Alikum

Aj hum ap ko Karachi ke sab se mashoor Beef Nehari banana seekha rahe hain!
✨ Ye commercial recipe hai! ✨

🔸 Beef Nehari Masala (مصالحہ جات) - For 2kg Beef:

  • 3 tukray sonth (har tukra 1 inch ka ho)
  • 3 adad pepli
  • 1 table spoon zeera
  • 1 tea spoon long
  • 1 tea spoon kali mirch
  • 4 tukray dar cheeni
  • 5 adad badiyan ke phool
  • 5 adad green choti elaichi
  • 2 adad badi elaichi
  • 1 tea spoon sonf
  • 1 tea spoon dhaniya powder

In sab masalon ko grind kar lein

  • 1.5 table spoon pisi lal mirch powder shamil karein
  • 1 tea spoon haldi shamil karein
  • 1.5 table spoon namak (hasb-e-zaroorat) shamil karein
💡 In tamam masalon ko achi tarah mix kar lein
Ab is mein 10 adad kali mirch (sabit) aur 10 adad long (sabit) bhi shamil karein
✅ 2kg Beef Nehari ke liye perfect tayyar masala hai!

🔸 Ingredients (اجزاء) - Beef Nehari:

  • 300 gram oil
  • 150 gram piyaz (bareek cut)
  • 2 table spoon adrak lehsan ka paste
  • 2kg beef (beef bong ka hona chahiye)
  • 3 liter pani

Atay ka mixture (Ayza):

  • 150 gram fine ata
  • 100 gram besan
  • 1/2 liter (aadha liter) pani

🔸 Beef Nehari banane ka tareeqa:

Step 1: Piyaz brown karein

  • Pateli ya deeg mein 300 gram oil daal kar garam karein
  • Jab oil garam ho jaye to is mein 150 gram piyaz (bareek cut) shamil kar ke brown karein

Step 2: Adrak lehsan shamil karein

  • Piyaz brown hone ke baad is mein 2 table spoon adrak lehsan ka paste shamil kar ke mix karein

Step 3: Beef fry karein

  • Ab is mein 2kg beef shamil karein
  • Achi tarah mix kar ke beef ko fry karein — takay beef ka colour change ho jaye aur beef ka apna pani bhi khushk ho jaye

Step 4: Nehari masala shamil karein

  • Ab is mein nehari masala shamil karein (jo hum ne pehle se tayyar kar ke rakha hai)
  • Achi tarah mix karein
💡 Halka sa pani lagate rahein — masala aur beef fry karte rahein
Ye tab tak karna hai jab tak piyaz phat na jaye aur dany daar gravy ban na jaye

Step 5: Pani shamil karein

  • Ab is mein 3 liter pani shamil karein
  • Chulha full kar dein — takay pani mein josh aajaye

Step 6: Atay ka mixture tayyar karein

  • Ek bartan mein 150 gram fine ata daalein
  • 100 gram besan shamil karein
  • 1/2 liter (aadha liter) pani shamil kar ke behtar tarah mix karein — takay koi ghotli na banay
⚠️ Important: Pani mein josh aa chuka hai — ab is mein ye ghol shamil karein aur chamch chalate rahein
Ghol ek hi baari mein sab na daalein — balkay thora thora kar ke shamil karein — warna neche se pakar lega!

Step 7: Chamch chalate rahein

  • Jab mukammal ghol shamil ho jaye to chamch chalate rahein
  • Is mein jhag aayegi — use nikalna nahi hai
  • Chulha medium rakhein aur tab tak chamch chalate rahein jab tak jhag khatam nahi ho jati
💡 Nehari la lob pakne ki yehi nishani hoti hai — ke uski jhag khatam ho jati hai, neche se nahi pakarti aur gravy full cheeni, laasdaar ho jati hai

Step 8: Dum lagaayein

  • Ab isay 1 ghanty (60 minute) ka dum lagaayein
  • Chulha low kar dein, dhakkan laga kar oper wazan rakh dein

Step 9: Dum ke baad check karein

  • 1 ghanty baad chak karein — beef bilkul naram hona chahiye
  • Itna naram ke boti hath mein pakrein to wo khud hi toot kar gir jaye
⚠️ Agar:
• Gravy patli ho ya beef naram na ho — to mazeed 30 minute ka dum lagaayein
• Beef naram nahi hua lekin gravy ready ho gayi hai — to aadha liter (full garam pani) shamil karein aur phir se dum par lagaayein

Step 10: Garam masala shamil karein

  • Ab is mein garam masala (jis mein zeera aur dhaniya powder shamil na ho — baqi mukammal masaly hon) 1 table spoon phela dein
  • Dum par rakhein

Step 11: Final check

  • Jab beef aur gravy ready ho jaye aur tamam oil upar aa jaye — to chulha band kar dein
⚠️ Khas Ehteyat (خاص احتیاط) - Beef Nehari:
• Agar 2kg beef bana rahe hain to 1 boti ka wazan 100 gram hona chahiye
• Agar 5kg bana rahe hain to 1 boti ka wazan 300 gram hona chahiye
• Jitni badi boti hogi — dum ka time utna zyada hoga

📌 Baz jaga par: Beef ki bhoonai nahi hoti, na he piyaz shamil hota hai — balki tamam masaly, oil, atay ka ghol ek saath shamil kar ke 2-3 ghanty dum par lagate hain — lekin ap aisa na karein!
Nehari par mehnat karein — taste se banayein!

Step 12: Garnish karein

  • Nehari ko plate mein daalein
  • Upar adrak (bareek lambi cut) phela dein
  • Hari mirch (cut ki hui) phela dein
  • Lemon cut kar ke rakh dein

✅ Ap ke bohat he lazeez Beef Nehari ready hai!
